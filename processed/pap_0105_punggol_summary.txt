**Summary of PAP Punggol GRC Election Rally (Singapore GE2025) – Main Points**

**Introduction & Candidates**
- The rally was held in Punggol GRC for the People’s Action Party (PAP).
- The candidates running: Gan Kim Yong (Deputy Prime Minister), Janil Puthucheary, Sun Xueling, Yeo Wan Ling.
- PM Lawrence Wong and several guest speakers (including Minister Indranee Rajah, Zaqy Mohamad, Lim Boon Heng) participated.

---

# Key Themes & Messages

## **1. Track Record and Local Improvements**

### **Town & Infrastructure Plans**
- Punggol has become a vibrant, sought-after residential area due to two decades of PAP government planning and investment.
- Key *upcoming and ongoing improvements*:
  - **Transport:** New two-car LRT, Punggol Coast Bus Interchange (opening June), maintaining old bus interchange, future connection to two MRT lines, new road extensions to reduce congestion.
  - **Amenities:** Large new sports stadium and facilities opening in 2026; expansion of Punggol Digital District (creating over 28,000 jobs).
  - **Childcare/Education:** 14 new childcare centers since 2020 (3,000+ new places), more to come; new programs for infant care, lower preschool costs.
  - **Community Support:** Active Aging Centers for seniors, fresh food distribution, community bonding events and youth mental health initiatives.

## **2. Support for Jobs, Workers, and Families**
- Emphasis on supporting workers, career resilience, jobs for all ages, and future-readiness.
- Partnerships with NTUC and Institutes like SIT to create job fairs, skills workshops (including AI/digital economy), and entrepreneurship for mid-career workers and caregivers.
- New career marketplace every two months; outreach has helped hundreds secure jobs.
- New flexible job options, micro-job initiatives to help parents/caregivers re-enter workforce.

## **3. Social Policy & Community Building**
- Special focus on needs of young families, elderly, and vulnerable residents:
  - Lowering infant milk/formula costs (task force led by Sun Xueling).
  - Child minding pilots, extended paternity/shared parental leave, improved healthcare.
- Importance of building local community spirit, reducing loneliness and social isolation, and fostering inclusiveness.

## **4. Emphasis on Action, Not Promises**
- PAP campaigns peacefully, without personal attacks or negative tactics.
- Promises to listen, act, deliver concrete solutions, and not just raise issues in Parliament.

## **5. Leadership & Stability**
- Strong personal endorsement of DPM Gan Kim Yong (“Task Force Man”) as an experienced leader who led COVID-19 response and has a reputation for dependability and humility.
- Importance of continuity and strong leadership in uncertain global times.
- Calls for a strong mandate for PM Lawrence Wong’s 4G team, noting many senior leaders have recently retired; new faces will need time.

## **6. Political Choices & Stakes**
- The Workers’ Party (WP) is criticized for a lack of firm positions on key issues and negative campaigning.
- The PAP highlights principled decision-making (e.g., repealing 377A while protecting marriage definition, steering Singapore through crises).
- PAP argues that voting for opposition will not only add more alternative voices (already guaranteed) but risks weakening the government’s ability to serve and defend Singapore’s interests.
- The need for political stability for continued foreign investment, jobs, and long-term planning.

## **7. Appeal to Voters**
- Choice framed as between “unity vs. division,” “real action vs. posturing,” and “proven track record vs. untested alternatives.”
- Urges residents to consider the character, track record, and commitment of PAP candidates.
- PM Wong emphasizes the PAP’s commitment to honest, transparent governance, owning up to mistakes, and always putting Singaporeans’ interests first.
- Call for support/votes for the PAP, with affirmations that the team will always prioritize Punggol residents and Singapore.

---

**Conclusion**
- Rally ends with thank-yous from all speakers.
- Urges residents to stay calm before polling day and make a considered choice.
- Strong final appeal from PM Wong and team: support PAP for a stable, caring, and forward-looking Singapore and Punggol.

---

**Core Message:**  
**PAP promises continued, practical improvements and strong leadership for Punggol and Singapore, highlights its proven track record, and warns against weakening stable leadership in uncertain times. Residents are urged to support the PAP team for real results and a brighter, united future for Singapore.**
