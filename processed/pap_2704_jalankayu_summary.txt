**Summary: PAP 2025 Election Rally at Jalan Kayu SMC (Ang Mo Kio Family)**

**Background & Setting:**
- The rally was held at Jalan Kayu Single Member Constituency (SMC), part of the extended “Ang Mo Kio family” of constituencies, to support PAP’s candidate Ng Chee Meng and his slate.
- The event featured energetic crowd interactions, reminders about safety and cleanliness, and encouragement for attendees to forge community bonds.

**Key Speakers & Major Themes:**

1. **Event Introduction (MC and Housekeeping):**
   - Warm welcome with lively cheers and flag-waving.
   - Emphasis on cleanliness, safety, and making new friends.
   - Encouraged taking photos/videos to spread positive energy on social media.

2. **Party Spirit and Community Unity:**
   - Repeated reminders that PAP is a “white party” standing for cleanliness and responsibility.
   - Calls for audience participation in cheers, rallying cries (“Majulah Singapura”), and connecting with neighbors from surrounding constituencies.
   - Emphasis on the importance of unity and community spirit across Ang Mo Kio, Jalan Kayu, Kebun Baru, Yio Chu Kang, Sengkang, and beyond.

3. **Candidate Introductions & Main Speeches:**
   
   a) **Darryl David (Ang Mo Kio GRC):**
      - Spoke of PAP MPs as effective, not yes-men; emphasized active debate and pushing for change in Parliament.
      - Cited his achievements in town council and educational policy.

   b) **Dr Lam Pin Min (Sengkang):**
      - Shared his personal journey, previous defeat, and determination to return and serve Sengkang.
      - Criticized the opposition’s “buy one, get one free” narrative (expecting PAP’s grassroots work even if voting for opposition).
      - Emphasized good governance, practical teamwork, and the “sweet spot” of PAP-led constituency management with NCMP opposition voices in Parliament.

   c) **Henry Kwek (Kebun Baru):**
      - Focused on caring for the elderly and vulnerable, showcasing local programs like “iDeal @ 115.”
      - Advocated for steady, honest governance and practical, scalable solutions.

   d) **Yip Hon Weng (Yio Chu Kang):**
      - Stressed real ground-up care (e.g., community shop, meal initiatives, free vaccinations).
      - Rejected claims that PAP MPs are silent, highlighting speeches and action both publicly and behind the scenes.

   e) **Jasmine Loo (Ang Mo Kio):**
      - Spoke as a mother and public servant about building a brighter Singapore for future generations.
      - Promised to listen, debate, and work as a team.

   f) **Victor Lye (joining Ang Mo Kio, ex-Aljunied):**
      - Shared experiences enduring toxic opposition in an opposition ward.
      - Called for unifying Singapore politics and putting people at the center.

   g) **Nadia Samdin (Ang Mo Kio):**
      - Called for kind, inclusive politics and the rejection of divisive or foreign-influenced political tactics.
      - Spoke on youth, women, and plural representation in Parliament.

   h) **Union Leader Abdul Samad:**
      - Defended the role and achievements of union leaders (including Ng Chee Meng) in improving workers’ lives.

   i) **Ng Chee Meng (PAP candidate, Jalan Kayu SMC):**
      - Shared why he is running again: a sense of duty following his 2020 loss.
      - Recapped frontline efforts during COVID-19, labor reform, and his promise to deliver for Jalan Kayu residents.
      - Criticized Workers’ Party for lacking constituency plans and for undervaluing labor voices in Parliament.
      - Detailed constituent engagement stories and promised continuity for successful community initiatives.

   j) **Senior Minister Lee Hsien Loong (Guest of Honour):**
      - Zoomed out to macro issues: global instability, economic risks, rising costs of living.
      - Praised PAP’s ability to deliver support (e.g., “$5,000” in government help per family).
      - Emphasized that good governance depends on voting in capable ministers/MPs, warning against "strategic voting" to “balance” Parliament, and dismissing opposition claims that more opposition equals better Parliament.
      - Explained the income-Alleanz issue as an example of PAP's transparent, effective governance (contrasting with WP’s lack of engagement).
      - Addressed recent concerns about race/religion in politics, warning against foreign interference and emphasizing Singapore's unique multi-racial model.
      - Called for fresh support for the new generation of PAP leaders, including Lawrence Wong, to ensure continued progress.

**Conclusion:**
- Rally closed with unity cheers (“Majulah Singapura,” “Majulah PAP”).
- Emphasis on responsible civic behavior, cleanliness, and readiness for the upcoming election’s challenges.

**Core Messages:**
- **PAP track record of stability, action, and unity.**
- **Candidates’ personal dedication and community achievements.**
- **Critique of opposition’s theoretical plans and strategic voting.**
- **Warning against divisive politics—especially along lines of race, religion, or foreign influence.**
- **Call for continued trust and support to maintain Singapore’s progress in a troubled world, via strong local leadership (Ng Chee Meng) and a capable PAP team.**
