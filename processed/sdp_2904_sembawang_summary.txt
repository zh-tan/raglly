**Summary of Singapore Democratic Party (SDP) Rally at Sembawang GRC – GE2025**

**Context:**  
This is a rally held by the Singapore Democratic Party (SDP) at Sembawang GRC for the 2025 General Election. The team is led by key members including Chee Soon Juan, Dr. Paul Tambaya, and others.

---

### Key Themes and Messages

#### 1. **Appreciation and Camaraderie**
- SDP expresses strong gratitude to supporters, volunteers, donors, and rally attendees.
- Emphasis on shared struggle (standing in the rain), mutual support, and the long-term loyalty of supporters.

#### 2. **Merchandise and Fundraising**
- Merchandise selling (e.g., shirts, teddy bears, books) is a crucial grassroots fundraising tool.
- Responded to online criticism of members selling merchandise—framing it as necessary and dignified, rather than shameful.
- Emphasis that election campaigns are expensive and require public support instead of relying on big donors or GLC funding.

#### 3. **Critique of PAP & Minister Ong Ye Kung**
- Criticized the incumbent PAP for:
    - Raising GST during tough times.
    - Rising cost of living and stagnant wages.
    - Prioritizing big business and MNCs over local businesses and citizens.
    - Using state resources and development projects as leverage to win votes.
    - Employing fear-mongering tactics (e.g., warnings about losing ministers if PAP is voted out).
    - Accusing them of copying SDP policies after resisting them (e.g., unemployment insurance, minimum wage, different tax structures).

#### 4. **SDP Policies and Vision**
- **Housing:**
    - Advocated removing land cost from HDB flat pricing (the "non-open market" flats), to make homes more affordable—SDP claims to have proposed this earlier than PSP.
    - Proposal for rent-to-own schemes.
    - Abolishing ABSD for Singaporeans buying a second property (for the "sandwich class").

- **Healthcare:**
    - Proposes a single-payer, universal health system to cut complexity, reduce costs, and focus resources on direct care.
    - Nationalize mental health care to address rising mental health issues.

- **Town Improvement:**
    - Criticism of PAP for election-timed upgrading; promises practical, people-first improvements.
    - Prioritize lifts for overhead bridges, more resting areas for elderly, accessible public work/study/cowork spaces, and creation of youth mental wellness centers.
    - Environmental commitments: preserve local beaches, improve water quality, and fund animal welfare initiatives.

- **Economy & Businesses:**
    - Support SMEs and local businesses through fairer rent policies and government support.
    - Capping rent increases on government-owned properties to stabilize costs for businesses and consumers.
    - Emphasize social safety nets and economic security over pure GDP growth.

- **Civil Service & Voting:**
    - Reassures civil servants and all voters that their vote is secret—urges voting with conscience, not fear.
    - Stresses that civil service serves the nation, not any single party; voting for opposition is not disloyalty.

#### 5. **Town Council Management**
- SDP presents their team as highly qualified and ready to take over Sembawang Town Council, emphasizing good governance, transparency, and continuity in public services.

#### 6. **Vision for Singapore**
- Proposes a Singapore in which everyone—not just the rich—can "thrive, not just survive."
- Cites Finland as a model for happiness—calls for a more equitable, people-oriented, and compassionate society with robust safety nets, affordable homes, fair opportunities, and active citizen participation.

---

### Rally Atmosphere and Closing

- The rally was interactive and lively—incorporating games, cheers, merchandise giveaways, and vibrant crowd engagement.
- Repeated reminders for voters that their votes are secret and that they should vote without fear.
- Calls for more volunteers, especially as polling agents.
- Rally closes with a call to action: vote for SDP and make real change for Sembawang and Singapore.

---

**Overall:**  
The SDP rallies for support in Sembawang GRC by portraying itself as an honest, courageous, people-first alternative to the PAP. The party highlights its track record of proposing policies later adopted by the government, presents detailed proposals on housing, healthcare, and economic reform, and appeals strongly to both hearts and minds—emphasizing community, fairness, and the need to elect a credible, compassionate opposition for real change.

**Main Slogan:**  
*"Thrive, not just survive."*  
*"Vote SDP on 3rd of May. Your vote is secret, do not fear."*
