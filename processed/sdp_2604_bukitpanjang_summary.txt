**Summary: Singapore Democratic Party (SDP) Election Rally at Bukit Panjang GRC (Context: 2025 General Election, candidate: Paul Anantharajah Tambyah, focus: Bukit Panjang GRC & Sambawang West)**

**Rally Opening & Operations:**  
- Rally began with welcomes, thanks to supporters and volunteers for braving bad weather.
- Emphasized need for donations and merchandise sales to fund the campaign and called for volunteers to serve as polling and counting agents.
- Repeatedly reassured attendees that “your vote is secret,” encouraging support for the SDP.

**Opening Speech (Joshua, Young Democrat):**  
- Shared his personal story as a second-generation Singaporean facing common fears of participating in opposition politics.
- Highlighted the struggles of ordinary Singaporeans like "Mr. Tan," an elderly cleaner unable to retire due to high living costs, medical expenses, and insufficient assistance.
- Criticized the PAP’s approach to senior citizens, rising costs, CPF policy, high HDB prices, and the suggestion people work longer into old age.
- Denounced government reliance on rebates and vouchers instead of implementing sustainable cost-of-living solutions.
- Called for change, quoting Gandhi and urging voters to remember the vulnerable as a measure of society.
- Challenged the narrative that affordable housing undermines reserves, suggesting keeping money with people benefits the economy more broadly.
- Shifted criticism towards elite mentalities, ministers’ salaries, and what he viewed as misplaced government priorities.
- Endorsed Dr. Chee Soon Juan as a dedicated candidate who listens to and helps residents, and encouraged support for SDP.

**Other SDP Candidates’ Speeches (Summaries):**
- **Jeffrey**:  
  - Criticized ministers’ million-dollar salaries and “useless” mayor positions.
  - Advocated cutting government waste and channeling funds towards health, education, and real support for Singaporeans.
  - Delivered part of the speech in Malay, highlighting integrity and support for the Malay community.

- **Dr. Gigin Wong:**  
  - Speech included segments in Chinese and English.
  - Focused on the need for more women in parliament, better civil service, more transparent governance, and overall improvement in the public sector.
  - Reiterated the need for volunteers and encouraged women in particular to step up.

- **Alec:**  
  - Emphasized the real daily struggles facing Singaporeans: cost of living, housing, and fairness.
  - Dismissed PAP narratives that the opposition is reckless.
  - Called for voting SDP for meaningful, people-oriented representation.

- **James Gomez:**  
  - Unveiled the SDP's Sambawang GRC Town Plan: “Putting the heart in the heartland.”
  - Criticized PAP for politicking with national development projects, taking credit for national plans, and failing to resolve local issues like maintenance, crowding, and unusable public spaces.
  - Detailed SDP’s proposals: secure bicycle storage, co-working spaces, more benches with support, lifts on overhead bridges, wellness centers, protection of green spaces, clean coastal waters, and animal welfare support.
  - Emphasized community-driven planning and regular town halls.

- **Brian Lin:**  
  - Emphasized the need for courageous, full-time representation, not “yes-men” MPs or token election-time efforts.
  - Strongly endorsed Dr. Chee, referencing his history of perseverance in the face of government pressure or legal actions.
  - Called for resistance against rising costs and for hope and dignity in politics.

**Supporting & Keynote Speech by Prof. Paul Tambyah (SDP Chair):**  
- Praised Dr. Chee as a conviction politician and highlighted his decades of commitment and sacrifice.
- Rebutted PAP’s “personal attacks” and shifting accusations over constituency moves, asking for a higher standard of ethical campaigning.
- Criticized the government’s use of “identity politics” and called for scrapping the divisive CMIO (race classification) system.
- Defended SDP’s call for a universal living wage over complicated PAP models, pointing out that Singaporeans want jobs that pay fairly.
- Outlined the third pillar of SDP’s manifesto: “Respect for our planet,” with detailed calls for action on climate change, shifting away from fossil fuels, careful stewardship of green spaces, and ground-up environmental action.

**Dr. Chee Soon Juan’s Speech:**  
- Described the pain and frustration of seeing Bukit Batok SMC dissolved and his years of community work there apparently erased.
- Recounted his efforts for the community (fixing safety issues, providing bursaries, and direct service — all funded by donations, not public money).
- Criticized PAP politicians for personal attacks and hypocrisy regarding candidates switching constituencies.
- Urged rivals to engage in a reputable, issues-based campaign, and invited them for an open conversation.
- Promised continued dedication to Sambawang West, with his team visiting every block and distributing flyers in a short time.
- Promised that, if elected, he will be a full-time MP: personally managing the town council (not outsourcing), prioritizing access for residents, regular meet-the-people sessions, and holding community town halls to decide on local issues.
- Highlighted the lack of open spaces in the estate, emphasizing links between mental health and urban planning, and criticized PAP for playing down these concerns.
- Emphasized that if elected, he’ll advocate for more open green spaces, mental health support, and ensure residents’ voices drive development.
- Pointed out his organizational strength and ability to surpass current town management.
- Reassured residents that necessary upgrading and municipal projects would continue regardless of who wins.
- Concluded with a passionate pledge: to serve as a full-time, present, and empathetic MP dedicated to fostering a caring and cohesive community, and urged residents to vote confidently for SDP as a vote for substantive change, compassion, and hope.

**Key Rally Themes:**
- Cost of living, affordable housing, retirement adequacy, and the plight of the lower income and elderly.
- Critique of the current government’s priorities, high salaries, and “profit-seeking” approach.
- Calls for transparency, accountability, and people-centered policies.
- Environmental stewardship and sustainable development as a pillar of SDP’s platform.
- Grassroots, full-time MP commitment (especially from Dr. Chee) and participatory town management.
- Shift away from fear-based and divisive politics toward dignity, hope, and real representation.

**Conclusion:**  
The SDP rally called for a “people first” approach, policy substance over personalities, and called on residents to vote for real change and community empowerment. They presented Dr. Chee Soon Juan and their team as principled, hardworking, and empathetic directly addressing the everyday struggles of Singaporeans and critiquing the ruling PAP’s approach to housing, wages, urban development, and political discourse.
