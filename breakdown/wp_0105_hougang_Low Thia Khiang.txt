**Summary of Low Thia Khiang’s Speech at the Workers’ Party GE2025 Rally (Hougang GRC)**

Low Thia Khiang, the former Secretary-General of the Workers’ Party (WP) and long-serving MP for Hougang and Aljunied, delivered a short but symbolic closing speech at the final WP rally for GE2025. His remarks were in line with the party’s tradition and served as a capstone to the campaign.

**Key Points from Low Thia Khiang’s Speech:**

- **Tradition and Continuity:**  
  Low highlighted that the Workers’ Party’s campaign did not just begin nine days ago, but has been ongoing for five years. He emphasized the party’s long-standing commitment to serving Singaporeans and upholding its traditions.

- **Unity and the Singapore Pledge:**  
  In keeping with WP rally tradition, Low invited the candidates and the crowd to recite the Singapore Pledge together. This act was meant to reinforce unity, shared values, and the party’s vision for a democratic society based on justice and equality.

- **Closing the Rally:**  
  Low’s speech marked the formal end of the rally and the campaign period. He thanked supporters for their dedication and urged everyone to return home safely, reminding them to vote for the Workers’ Party on polling day.

**Tone and Message:**

- Low’s speech was brief, ceremonial, and focused on unity, tradition, and the collective spirit of the Workers’ Party and its supporters.
- He did not engage in policy debate or criticism, but instead provided a sense of closure and solidarity, rallying the crowd around the party’s core values and the importance of their vote.

**Conclusion:**

Low Thia Khiang’s role at the rally was to provide a dignified and unifying close to the Workers’ Party’s campaign, reminding supporters of the party’s enduring mission and the importance of participating in Singapore’s democratic process.
