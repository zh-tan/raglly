**Summary of He Ting Ru’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Rally (Hougang GRC, GE2025):**

He Ting Ru, the Workers’ Party (WP) candidate and incumbent MP for Sengkang GRC, delivered a heartfelt and reflective speech towards the end of the rally. Here are the key points she made:

1. **Reflection on the Past Five Years:**
   - She spoke about her journey as an MP, her work in Parliament, the town council, and the Sengkang neighborhood.
   - She described every day as a learning experience and an opportunity to better understand Singapore and its people.

2. **On Political Differences:**
   - He Ting Ru emphasized that people who disagree with the WP are not enemies but neighbors, friends, family, and fellow Singaporeans who also want Singapore to succeed.
   - She called for the freedom to challenge the PAP’s vision for Singapore’s future, stressing that the country’s story must be written together by all Singaporeans.

3. **Role of the Workers’ Party:**
   - She reminded the audience that even before Parliament was dissolved, the narrative from the PAP was about the need for a strong government in uncertain times.
   - She recalled how, during the pandemic, Singaporeans still voted for a responsible opposition to ensure their voices were heard.
   - The WP scrutinized government policies, supporting those that moved Singapore forward and speaking out when they disagreed.

4. **Facing Uncertainty with Courage:**
   - She argued that Singaporeans should not fear change or uncertainty, drawing parallels with the resilience shown during the pandemic.
   - The WP promises to step up and ask tough questions, not to remain silent or simply follow the PAP if elected again.

5. **Gratitude to Volunteers and Team:**
   - She expressed deep appreciation for the WP’s volunteers, who sacrifice personal time and family life to support the party and its mission.
   - She described the WP as a “village” that supports each other through challenges.

6. **Personal Motivation and Legacy:**
   - On a personal note, He Ting Ru shared how her children ask about her busy work, and she tells them she is working to write the story of the Singapore they will inherit.
   - She recounted her grandparents’ journey to Singapore, highlighting the bravery of past generations who faced uncertainty to build a better future.
   - She urged Singaporeans to be brave like the pioneers and to write the next chapter of Singapore’s story together.

7. **Call to Action:**
   - She called on voters to join the WP in writing Singapore’s story with love and hope, and to vote for the Workers’ Party on May 3rd.
   - She ended by reaffirming the WP’s commitment to work for Singapore and to help shape its future together with the people.

**In summary:**  
He Ting Ru’s speech was a call for unity, courage, and hope. She stressed the importance of having a responsible opposition, learning from the past, and working together—across political lines—to write Singapore’s next chapter. She appealed to voters to support the Workers’ Party, not out of fear, but out of hope for a better, more inclusive Singapore.
