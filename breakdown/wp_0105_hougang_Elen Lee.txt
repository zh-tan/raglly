**Summary of Elen Lee's Speech at the Workers' Party Rally (Hougang GRC, GE2025):**

Elen Lee, a candidate for the Workers’ Party (WP), delivered a reflective and forward-looking speech at the rally. She began by acknowledging the emotional journey of the campaign and thanked the crowd for their support. Drawing inspiration from the National Day song "The Road Ahead," Elen emphasized the importance of Singapore’s collective journey—honoring the past while looking boldly to the future.

**Key Points from Elen Lee’s Speech:**

1. **Learning from History:**  
   - Elen highlighted the Singapore story, noting that the nation’s progress was built on hard work and difficult choices.
   - She recalled how Singapore’s first-generation leaders had fierce debates but always prioritized the country’s best interests, making bold decisions that shaped today’s Singapore.

2. **Role of the Workers’ Party:**  
   - She stated that WP MPs embody this same spirit in Parliament, speaking up for Singaporeans and pushing for policies that benefit all.
   - Elen cited WP’s advocacy for:
     - More financial support for the involuntarily unemployed.
     - Flexible work arrangements and more shared parental leave.
     - Expanding public rental schemes and reducing BTO waiting times.
   - She stressed that when good ideas become policy, everyone wins, regardless of which party proposed them.

3. **The Importance of Debate and Honest Conversations:**  
   - Elen argued that progress comes from hard work and difficult, honest conversations—not evasion.
   - She encouraged voters to keep the conversation going, ensuring thoughtful debates that lead to meaningful action and effective solutions.

4. **Looking to the Future:**  
   - She posed the question: “How can Singapore continue to improve?” and answered that it requires courage—courage to listen to different voices and to vote for the Singapore we want to see.
   - Elen reminded the audience that their vote is not just about the next five years, but about the kind of Singapore they want for their children.

5. **Call to Action:**  
   - She concluded by urging Singaporeans to build a nation that honors its past while embracing the future with courage.
   - Elen called on voters to support the Workers’ Party, emphasizing that WP is “Working for Singapore.”

**In essence, Elen Lee’s speech was a call for unity, courage, and thoughtful progress, urging Singaporeans to vote for hope, honest debate, and a better future with the Workers’ Party.**
