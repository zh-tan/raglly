**Summary of Dr. Ang Yiling’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Rally (Singapore GE2025, Hougang GRC):**

Dr. Ang Yiling, a psychologist by training and a candidate for Tampines GRC, delivered a speech focused on her vision and approach as a potential Member of Parliament. Here are the key points from her speech:

1. **Background & Approach:**
   - Dr. Ang introduced herself as a psychologist and public servant, emphasizing her experience in helping people grow, families heal, and youth thrive.
   - She believes that when people are supported, they can soar, and she wants Tampines to soar as well.

2. **Community Strength:**
   - She praised the Tampines community for its heart, resilience, and pride, noting the strong sense of togetherness among residents.

3. **Leadership Style:**
   - Dr. Ang highlighted her approach: listening before acting, solving problems with care and empathy, and focusing on long-term solutions rather than quick fixes.
   - She pledged to bring these values, along with her teammates Faizal, Elen, Jimmy, and Michael, to Parliament.

4. **Four Key Pledges for Tampines:**
   - **Building Stronger Communities:** Championing inclusive spaces for seniors, youth, and all residents, making every block feel like home.
   - **Responsive Services:** Improving town management with quicker updates, co-created solutions, and cleaner, greener spaces.
   - **Creating Wellness that Matters:** Prioritizing mental wellness as a necessity, not a luxury, and fostering neighborhoods where people feel safe and cared for.
   - **Being a Listening Leader:** Committing to listen to residents, letting their voices shape how the team serves, and using feedback to guide actions.

5. **Workers’ Party Values:**
   - Dr. Ang stressed that the Workers’ Party is not about arguing or attacking but about offering fresh ideas, balanced perspectives, and helping to build the country.
   - She emphasized the importance of bringing new voices—including those of residents—into national conversations.

6. **Empowerment & Representation:**
   - She argued that residents deserve more than just a vote; they deserve a voice and a seat at the parliamentary table, and a future they help shape.

7. **Vision for the Future:**
   - Dr. Ang called for a future where every child has a chance, every senior feels safe and connected, and every resident is proud to call Tampines home.
   - She urged voters to make their vote count for partnership, possibilities, and a better Singapore.

8. **Closing:**
   - She concluded by reaffirming her team’s readiness to listen, serve, and fight for residents, aiming to build an inclusive, fair, happy, and better Tampines and Singapore.

**In summary:**  
Dr. Ang Yiling’s speech centered on empathetic, community-focused leadership, the importance of mental wellness, responsive town management, and the need for residents’ voices in Parliament. She positioned the Workers’ Party as a constructive, listening, and inclusive force, inviting voters to help shape a better future together.
