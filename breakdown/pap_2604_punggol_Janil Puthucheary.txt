### Summary of Janil Puthucheary’s Speech at PAP Punggol GRC Rally

**Dr. Janil Puthucheary** began by emphasizing the seriousness of the upcoming election and the importance of the future of Punggol. He acknowledged that while he is known for being approachable and joking with children, tonight’s topic is about the serious business of shaping Punggol’s future.

#### Achievements and Progress in Punggol
- **Rapid Growth and Development:** Janil highlighted the significant changes and developments in Punggol over the past 15 years, such as the opening of One Punggol, Northshore Plaza, Oasis Terraces, the regional library, Sengkang SAFRA, Coney Island Park, and the linear parks. He noted that even residents who have only been in Punggol for a few years would have noticed the rapid pace of change.
- **Challenges and Solutions:** He acknowledged that rapid growth brings challenges—noise, congestion, dust, and delays—but assured residents that with patience and hard work, these issues are being addressed. He specifically mentioned ongoing improvements to telco connectivity in new BTOs and the commitment to fix remaining problems.
- **Community Bonds:** Janil stressed that building a better home is not just about physical infrastructure but about building strong community bonds—turning strangers into neighbors, and neighbors into friends. He said that these bonds help the community weather crises, as seen during COVID-19.

#### Inclusive and Community-Focused Facilities
- **Punggol Regional Library:** Janil pointed out that the library is not just a beautiful building, but a model of inclusivity, with programs for seniors, children with special needs, and mother tongue reading programs.
- **Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) and Punggol Digital District:** He described how SIT and the Punggol Digital District were developed in close partnership with the community, bringing 28,000 new tech jobs and integrating students into the Punggol family. He highlighted the unique features, such as the student canteen by the waterfront, and the opportunities these developments bring for residents.
- **Linear Parks:** Janil recounted the transformation of unused land under LRT tracks into vibrant linear parks, sports courts, and playgrounds. He explained the process of working with community leaders, overcoming regulatory hurdles, and learning from both successes and setbacks to continually improve these shared spaces.

#### Approach to Leadership and Governance
- **Pragmatic and Responsible Promises:** Janil contrasted the PAP’s approach—making careful, solemn promises and delivering on them—with opposition parties’ “flashy slogans” and “unbelievable promises.” He emphasized that the PAP’s duty is to deliver on what is promised, and that residents should hold them accountable.
- **Community Engagement:** He described how the PAP team, including himself, Sun Xueling, and Yeo Wan Ling, have been actively building networks, identifying needs, and solving problems for residents, not just during election season but every day.

#### Looking Forward
- **Upcoming Projects:** Janil mentioned the upcoming Punggol Sports Centre and the ongoing efforts to activate community spaces for sports and fitness even before the new facilities are ready.
- **Continuous Improvement:** He assured residents that the work is ongoing and that the team is committed to completing what they have started, implementing new programs, and making Punggol an even better home.

#### Closing
- **Call for Support:** Janil ended by expressing hope that residents would give the PAP team the mandate to continue their work, emphasizing the importance of trust, accountability, and community partnership in building Punggol’s future.

Janil Puthucheary’s speech focused on the tangible progress made in Punggol under the PAP, the importance of community bonds, the responsible and pragmatic approach of the PAP to promises and governance, and the commitment to continuous improvement and inclusivity.
