**Summary of Kenneth Tong’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Rally (Hougang GRC, GE2025):**

Kenneth Tong, running as a Workers’ Party (WP) candidate for Aljunied GRC, focused his rally speech on the sense of unease many Singaporeans feel about their future. He highlighted several key issues:

### 1. **Rising Cost of Living and Economic Insecurity**
- Kenneth described the anxiety Singaporeans face as the cost of food, housing (BTO, HDB resale, rents), and public transport rises faster than salaries.
- He noted the job market’s uncertainty, with people fearing retrenchment, struggling to find new jobs, and fresh graduates facing difficulties.

### 2. **Challenges Facing Singapore**
- He identified two major challenges:
  - The global technology revolution: Singapore risks becoming just a consumer, not a creator, of new technologies, causing local talent to go overseas.
  - A changing world order: With protectionism and global conflicts, Singapore needs foresight and strong domestic capabilities.

### 3. **Need for a Stronger Social Safety Net**
- Kenneth argued that Singaporeans are often criticized for being risk-averse, but this is understandable given the lack of a robust safety net.
- He called for:
  - Unemployment support and mandatory retrenchment benefits.
  - A national minimum wage.
  - Independent unions and strong workplace fairness laws.
- He said these measures would give Singaporeans the security to take risks, innovate, and pursue their dreams.

### 4. **Building Local Champions and Innovation**
- Kenneth questioned the effectiveness of Singapore’s decades of R&D investment, noting the lack of globally successful, homegrown tech companies.
- He compared Singapore to Denmark, which, despite its small size, has produced global giants like Lego and Novo Nordisk.
- He criticized the PAP’s willingness to sell national assets (e.g., Chartered Semiconductor, Neptune Orient Lines, the attempted sale of NTUC Income) to foreign buyers, arguing this undermines Singapore’s ability to build its own economic champions.

### 5. **Policy Proposals and Commitments**
If elected, Kenneth pledged to champion:
  1. Making Singapore a leader in innovation and creating good jobs locally.
  2. Strengthening economic security and fairness for all families.
  3. Supporting strong family foundations, affordable housing, high education standards, and better support for families (including those with foreign spouses).

### 6. **Call to Action**
- Kenneth urged voters to reject the ideology of selling out local assets and instead support policies that grow Singapore’s own enterprises and capabilities.
- He called on Singaporeans to lead the country into a better future by voting for the Workers’ Party, which he said stands for building strong foundations and always working for Singapore.

**In summary:**  
Kenneth Tong’s speech was a call for a new direction—one that prioritizes a stronger social safety net, homegrown innovation, and economic security for Singaporeans. He criticized the PAP’s track record on nurturing local champions and urged voters to support the Workers’ Party for a more balanced, resilient, and hopeful Singapore.
