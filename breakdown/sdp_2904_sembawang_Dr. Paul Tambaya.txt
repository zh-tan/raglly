**Summary of Dr. Paul Tambaya’s Speech at the SDP Sembawang GRC Rally (Singapore Election 2025):**

Dr. Paul Tambaya, the SDP candidate for Bukit Panjang SMC and a key leader in the party, delivered a thoughtful and visionary speech at the Sembawang GRC rally. Here are the main points he made:

---

### 1. **Campaign Is Not Personal, It’s About Vision**
- Dr. Tambaya emphasized that the SDP’s campaign is not a personal attack on Minister Ong Ye Kung or the PAP, but about offering Singaporeans a different vision for the future.
- He recounted a cordial encounter with Ong Ye Kung, highlighting mutual respect and that political competition should not be personal.

### 2. **A Vision for a Thriving Singapore**
- The SDP’s manifesto is built around the idea that **everyone should be able to thrive, not just survive**—not just the wealthy elite, but all Singaporeans.
- He contrasted the current system, where only a small minority truly thrive, with the SDP’s vision of a fairer society.

### 3. **What Would a Thriving Singapore Look Like?**
Dr. Tambaya painted a picture of a Singapore where:
- **Maternal and child health** is universally provided, as in Singapore’s “golden era” of public health.
- **Children** receive equal access to quality education, with smaller class sizes, less administrative burden on teachers, and the abolishment of the PSLE.
- **Higher education and scholarships** are accessible to all Singaporeans, not just foreigners.
- **Workers’ rights** are protected, including a living wage and fair competition for jobs.
- **SMEs** are supported to compete with MNCs.
- **Housing** is affordable, with options for non-open market flats (priced without land cost), so young families can start life without crippling debt.
- **Arts, sports, and culture** are valued and supported.
- **Social safety nets** are robust, with retrenchment insurance and senior support schemes so no elderly person needs to work menial jobs to survive.

### 4. **Policy Alternatives and Accountability**
- The SDP will hold the government accountable in Parliament, even if they are a minority, by presenting alternatives and identifying gaps in current policies.
- Dr. Tambaya stressed that the SDP has been consistent in its values for over 40 years, advocating for democratic socialism and policies that benefit the majority.

### 5. **Critique of the PAP’s Approach**
- He criticized the PAP for focusing on vouchers and handouts rather than systemic solutions to issues like inflation and cost of living.
- He addressed the “$1 meal” program, acknowledging its popularity but reminding residents that such programs are funded by taxpayers and should not be used as political carrots.

### 6. **On Losing Ministers and Democracy**
- Responding to PAP warnings about the risk of “losing ministers,” Dr. Tambaya argued that democracy means voters have the right to replace underperforming ministers with more competent, responsive representatives.
- If the PAP cannot function after losing a few ministers, that is a problem with their system, not with democracy.

### 7. **Call to Action**
- Dr. Tambaya urged voters to **vote without fear**, to choose change, and to support the SDP for a Singapore where everyone can thrive.
- He concluded by recognizing the contributions of activists and volunteers, and by reiterating the SDP’s commitment to serve all Singaporeans.

---

**In summary:**  
Dr. Paul Tambaya’s speech was a call for a more inclusive, compassionate, and visionary Singapore—one where every citizen, regardless of background, has the opportunity to thrive. He positioned the SDP as a principled, policy-driven alternative to the PAP, ready to hold the government accountable and to champion real change for ordinary Singaporeans.
