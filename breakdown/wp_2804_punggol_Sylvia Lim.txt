**Summary of Sylvia Lim’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Punggol GRC Rally (Singapore GE2025):**

Sylvia Lim, a veteran Workers’ Party (WP) MP and former party chair, focused her rally speech on the importance of women in politics, especially in the opposition, and highlighted the WP’s slate of female candidates for GE2025.

**Key Points:**

1. **Women in Opposition Politics:**
   - Sylvia Lim shared her personal experiences as a woman in opposition politics, describing it as a “high stakes, high-risk activity” that can be “nasty,” especially for women.
   - She noted the double standards faced by women MPs: expected to be robust in parliament but not “too aggressive.”
   - Despite being called names and attacked by ministers, she stood her ground and joked that her husband found these qualities attractive.

2. **Why Women Leaders Matter:**
   - Lim argued that having women in political leadership, especially in opposition, is essential for more balanced and sustainable policies.
   - She cited studies showing that countries with more women leaders tend to have happier citizens, as these societies prioritize social outcomes like work-life balance, health, and support for those in need.
   - She recalled being mocked by PAP MPs in 2011 for suggesting that Singapore should measure policies by happiness and well-being, but noted this is now international best practice.

3. **WP’s Female Candidates:**
   - Lim proudly introduced the six women WP is fielding this election, calling them “women of substance” who will “speak up for you fearlessly.”
   - She highlighted the credentials and qualities of:
     - **Alia Mattar (Punggol GRC):** Mother of three, lawyer, former MAS legal counsel, now in a multinational corporation.
     - **Alexis Dang (Punggol GRC):** Experienced in banking and tech, bilingual, understands young families’ and singles’ concerns.
     - **Ellen Cheong (Tampines GRC):** Former diplomat, now in philanthropy, focused on early childhood and youth mental health.
     - **Paris V. Paramaswari (East Coast GRC):** Navy veteran, worked in insurance and US Navy, strong volunteer spirit.
     - **He Ting Ru (Sengkang GRC):** Proven MP, mother of three, managed town council well.
     - **Herself (Aljunied GRC):** Longest-serving female opposition leader in Singapore.

   - Lim contrasted WP’s record with the PAP, noting she has outlasted four male PAP chairmen and that PAP has yet to have a female chair.

4. **What WP Stands For:**
   - The WP wants Singapore to succeed, for parents to age well, children to be safe and happy, and for everyone to reach their potential.
   - Opposition MPs can reject government policies not in Singaporeans’ interests, unlike PAP MPs.

5. **Urgency of This Election:**
   - Lim warned that if this batch of WP candidates is not elected, “there may be no next time” due to possible changes in electoral boundaries.
   - She urged voters not to “dither” but to seize the moment and vote WP now, as future opportunities may not come.

6. **Closing Call:**
   - Lim ended with a rallying cry: “Let May 3rd, 2025 be your moment. Vote for a stronger Singapore. Vote for the Workers’ Party. Working for Singapore.”

**In summary:**  
Sylvia Lim’s speech was a passionate call for greater female representation in parliament, especially from the opposition, and a strong endorsement of the WP’s women candidates. She emphasized the need for balanced, people-centric policies and urged voters to act decisively in this election, warning that the opportunity for change may not come again.
