**Summary of Daryl David’s Speech at the PAP Jalan Kayu GRC Rally (Singapore GE2025)**

Daryl David, a PAP candidate from the Ang Mo Kio family, delivered a speech focused on unity, service, and the importance of community at the Jalan Kayu rally. Here are the key points he made:

### 1. **Emphasis on Community and Unity**
- Daryl repeatedly referred to the “Ang Mo Kio family,” stressing that the PAP team is not just about individual constituencies but about working together as one united group.
- He welcomed supporters from Jalan Kayu, Kebun Baru, Yio Chu Kang, and Ang Mo Kio, as well as NTUC union members, highlighting the broad base of support and the interconnectedness of the constituencies.

### 2. **Track Record of Service**
- Daryl shared his experience as a Member of Parliament for Ang Mo Kio GRC for the past 10 years, particularly in the Ang Mo Kio-Hougang ward.
- He described his work in the Ang Mo Kio Town Council, first as vice-chairman and then as chairman, focusing on not just maintaining but enhancing the environment and driving town-wide initiatives.
- He cited the rejuvenation of the Ang Mo Kio Town Centre as an example of successful community improvement under his and his team’s leadership.

### 3. **PAP MPs Are Not “Yes Men”**
- Daryl addressed criticisms that PAP backbench MPs are ineffective or simply “yes men.” He strongly refuted this, stating that PAP MPs scrutinize, debate, and challenge government policies when necessary.
- He gave examples from his own parliamentary work, such as advocating for alternatives to the PSLE, professional development for teachers, and providing personal learning devices for students.

### 4. **Personal Commitment**
- Daryl expressed his privilege and honor in serving as an MP, emphasizing his commitment to continue speaking up and working for the community if re-elected.
- He highlighted the importance of building bonds and creating a sense of community through events, initiatives, and direct engagement with residents.

### 5. **Call for Support and Unity**
- He rallied the crowd to come together as one Ang Mo Kio family, encouraging everyone to support the PAP team and to vote for the party in the upcoming election.
- Daryl also introduced the next speaker, Dr. Lam Pin Min, describing him as a mentor, comrade, and friend, and praised his dedication and compassion.

### 6. **Interactive and Energetic Approach**
- Daryl’s speech was energetic and interactive, involving the audience in cheers and rallying cries, and encouraging them to connect with each other and show their support.

---

**In summary:**  
Daryl David’s speech centered on his track record of service, the importance of teamwork and unity within the PAP and the Ang Mo Kio family, and a strong rebuttal to claims that PAP MPs are ineffective. He called on residents to support the PAP for continued progress and stability, and to trust in their commitment to serve and speak up for the community.
