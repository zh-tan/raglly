**Summary of Brian Lim’s Speech at SDP Sembawang GRC Rally (Singapore Election 2025):**

Brian Lim, candidate for Sembawang GRC with the Singapore Democratic Party (SDP), delivered a speech focused on rebutting criticisms from PAP Minister Ong Ye Kung and highlighting the SDP’s history of policy innovation.

**Key Points from Brian Lim’s Speech:**

1. **Rebuttal to PAP’s Criticism:**
   - Brian directly addressed Minister Ong’s claim that the opposition (specifically SDP) only proposes “radical” policies, while the PAP is pragmatic and well-considered.
   - He challenged the audience to use their phones and ChatGPT to search for “How many SDP policies has the PAP copied and adopted in the last 30 years?”—demonstrating that many SDP proposals, once dismissed as radical, were later implemented by the PAP.

2. **SDP’s Track Record of Policy Innovation:**
   - Brian emphasized that the PAP has a history of criticizing SDP’s ideas during elections, only to adopt them later without giving credit.
   - He encouraged supporters to share this fact with Minister Ong and others, using technology and social media to spread awareness.

3. **Engagement and Humor:**
   - Brian used humor and interactive exercises (like the ChatGPT search) to engage the crowd and make his point memorable.
   - He joked about not asking the audience to collect cardboard (a reference to social issues in Singapore) and made light-hearted remarks about PAP ministers and their lifestyles.

4. **Call to Action:**
   - He reminded voters that their vote is secret and urged them to vote for SDP on 3rd May, so that SDP’s 11 candidates can be their voice in Parliament.
   - Brian reinforced the message that SDP candidates are ready to serve and bring real change, not just empty slogans.

5. **Overall Message:**
   - The SDP is not just about criticizing the government but has a proven record of proposing forward-thinking policies that eventually benefit Singaporeans.
   - Voters should not be swayed by PAP’s fear-mongering or dismissive rhetoric, but instead recognize the value of having SDP MPs in Parliament to push for meaningful reforms.

**In summary:**  
Brian Lim’s speech was a spirited defense of the SDP’s policy credibility, a direct rebuttal to PAP’s criticisms, and a rallying call for voters to support the SDP for real change and representation in Parliament. He used wit, technology, and facts to empower the audience and highlight the importance of their vote.
