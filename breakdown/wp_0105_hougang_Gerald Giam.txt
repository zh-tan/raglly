**Summary of Gerald Giam’s Speech at the Workers’ Party GE2025 Rally (Hougang GRC):**

Gerald Giam, candidate for Aljunied GRC, delivered a speech focused on the Workers’ Party’s (WP) track record, the importance of a balanced parliament, and addressing common concerns and misconceptions about voting for the opposition.

**Key Points from Gerald Giam’s Speech:**

1. **Track Record in Town Management:**
   - Gerald highlighted that all Aljunied and Hougang candidates, including himself, have served as town councillors, directly managing their town councils (AHTC and Sengkang) without relying on external managing agents, unlike most PAP town councils.
   - Both WP-run town councils ended the last term on solid financial footing and received all-green ratings in the Ministry of National Development’s latest Town Council Management Report.
   - He invited voters from outside WP wards to visit and see for themselves that WP estates are clean, orderly, and well-managed.

2. **Role in Parliament:**
   - Gerald emphasized that electing WP MPs is not just about town management, but about having independent voices in parliament who are not bound by the government whip (which requires PAP MPs to vote with their party even if they disagree).
   - He argued that the PAP voice is already dominant in parliament and the media, and what’s missing is the voice of ordinary Singaporeans—one that asks tough questions and proposes alternatives.

3. **Policy Advocacy:**
   - WP MPs have spoken up on issues such as cost of living, job security, housing affordability, healthcare, and caregiver support.
   - They have called for stronger worker protections, greater transparency, and accountability, with some of their efforts eventually shaping government policy.

4. **Vote Secrecy:**
   - Gerald directly addressed concerns about whether votes are secret, assuring voters that the process is secure and confidential, based on his experience contesting four general elections and observing the process until the incineration of ballot papers.

5. **Debunking Fallacies:**
   - He tackled three common misconceptions:
     - **Fallacy 1:** A stronger opposition will weaken the government. Gerald explained that even if WP wins all the seats it contests, the PAP will still have a supermajority and easily form the government.
     - **Fallacy 2:** More opposition means more fighting in parliament. He clarified that WP debates with facts and logic, not shouting or name-calling, and that more opposition means better scrutiny, not instability.
     - **Fallacy 3:** The opposition is only talk. Gerald pointed to WP’s 34 years of running town councils, their hands-on work during COVID-19, and their active parliamentary engagement (over 2,300 questions, 45 bills/motions, 34 ministerial statements in the last term).

6. **Call to Action:**
   - Gerald urged voters to share what they have seen and heard, and to convince undecided friends and family to vote for WP for a more balanced parliament and a better Singapore.
   - He concluded by stating that the WP is ready to serve new areas and that a vote for WP is a vote for a rational, responsible, and credible opposition.

**In summary:**  
Gerald Giam’s speech was a confident report card on WP’s performance in town management and parliament, a rebuttal of PAP’s fear-based arguments, and a strong call for voters to support the Workers’ Party to ensure a more balanced, accountable, and responsive government. He reassured voters about the secrecy of their vote and emphasized that a stronger opposition will not destabilize Singapore but will make it better.
