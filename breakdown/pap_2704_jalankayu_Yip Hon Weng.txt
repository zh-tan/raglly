### Summary of Yip Hon Weng’s Speech

**Yip Hon Weng**, PAP candidate for Yio Chu Kang SMC, focused his speech on the theme of *care*—not just as a policy, but as a way of life and a core value of the PAP.

#### Key Points:

- **Personal Encounters and Ground-Up Initiatives**:  
  Yip shared a story about meeting an elderly resident at "Kurdai," a free community shop in Yio Chu Kang, who thanked him for the help provided. He emphasized that such initiatives are not just government programs, but are built by residents, volunteers, and donors coming together to support one another.

- **Mission Since 2020**:  
  Since becoming MP, his mission has been to build a Yio Chu Kang where every resident feels supported and cared for. He linked this to the PAP’s values of service, solidarity, and standing with people in both good and tough times.

- **Cost of Living and Practical Support**:  
  Yip acknowledged that the cost of living is a major concern. He highlighted the PAP’s response through the Assurance Package, which included cash payouts, CDC vouchers, and MediSave top-ups—real help reaching real people.

- **Examples of Local Initiatives**:  
  - **Kurdai**: Residents can pick up essentials with no questions asked.
  - **Project Greenstripe**: Collected over 200kg of clothing for families in need.
  - **Free Vaccination Programs**: For seniors.
  - **Budget Meal Initiatives**: $1 meals and $3.50 hearty meals at participating stores.
  - **Dementia-Friendly Neighborhood**: Yio Chu Kang is Singapore’s first fully dementia-friendly neighborhood, supporting seniors to age with dignity.

- **Parliamentary Advocacy**:  
  Yip countered the claim that PAP backbenchers are “silent,” stating he has spoken up over 580 times in Parliament and delivered three speeches. He stressed that PAP MPs do the hard work, often behind the scenes, shaping and following through on solutions for residents.

- **Examples of Policy Impact**:  
  - Pushed for higher MediSave withdrawal limits, which were implemented.
  - Advocated for more ServiceSG centers—now there are nine across Singapore.
  - Improved multilingual communication for seniors.

- **PAP’s Approach**:  
  Yip emphasized that PAP MPs stay close to residents, build better policies together, and show up not just during elections but whenever it matters.

- **Call to Action**:  
  He acknowledged that the road ahead will be challenging, but called for trust in the PAP’s values of care, commitment, and community. He urged residents to vote for the PAP to ensure no one is left behind and that Singapore moves forward together.

- **Closing Rally Cry**:  
  He ended with a rallying call:  
  > “Vote for the People’s Action Party. Vote for a Singapore that leaves no one behind. Vote for a Singapore that moves forward together. Majulah Singapura!”

Yip Hon Weng’s speech was centered on the PAP’s record and commitment to caring for residents through practical support, community initiatives, and active parliamentary advocacy. He highlighted the importance of solidarity, real action, and trust in the PAP to deliver for all Singaporeans, especially in challenging times.
