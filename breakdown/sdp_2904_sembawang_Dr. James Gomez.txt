**Summary of Dr. James Gomez’s Speech at the SDP Sembawang GRC Rally (Singapore Election 2025)**

Dr. James Gomez, SDP candidate for Sembawang GRC, delivered a speech titled **“Reset Sembawang: Replace PAP, Install Change.”** Here are the key points he made:

---

### 1. **Call for Change and Reset**
- Dr. Gomez urged Sembawang voters to use their vote on 3rd May as a chance to “reset” Sembawang by replacing the PAP with the SDP.
- He criticized the PAP for being “stuck in the past,” relying on old fear-mongering tactics, and failing to bring real change or new ideas.

### 2. **SDP as a First-Tier Opposition**
- He acknowledged that the SDP is now seen as a first-tier opposition party, but emphasized that the party is made up of ordinary, passionate people.
- The SDP is contesting Sembawang GRC at the request of residents, aiming to serve with dedication.

### 3. **Critique of PAP’s Record and Tactics**
- Dr. Gomez accused the PAP, and specifically Minister Ong Ye Kung, of:
    - Taking credit for public infrastructure and development that are actually funded by taxpayers, not party gifts.
    - Issuing veiled threats that Sembawang will lose development if it doesn’t vote PAP.
    - Lacking transparency and accountability, and being out of touch with residents’ needs.
    - Using fear to discourage voters from supporting the opposition.

### 4. **SDP’s Alternative Vision and Town Plan**
- He highlighted that the SDP has already published a detailed, people-centered town plan for Sembawang, focusing on inclusivity, accessibility, and community needs (e.g., more seating for seniors, lifts on overhead bridges, youth support, environmental protection).
- In contrast, PAP’s Sembawang plan was still not published late into the campaign.

### 5. **Advocacy for Real Public Engagement**
- Dr. Gomez criticized the PAP for mocking SDP’s proposal for regular town halls, calling it “political theater.”
- He argued that town halls are essential for real engagement and accountability, and SDP will hold them regularly.

### 6. **Policy Proposals**
- **Healthcare:** Criticized the complexity and cost of the current system under Health Minister Ong, who lacks a medical background. SDP proposes a single-payer universal healthcare model to reduce costs and administrative waste, and to make mental health care a national priority.
- **Housing:** Rebutted PAP’s claim that removing land cost from BTO flats would harm resale values. Emphasized that young people want affordable homes, not speculative assets. SDP’s “non-open market” scheme would price flats based on construction/admin costs, with HDB buybacks to ensure affordability.
- **Accountability:** Promised regular town halls and open engagement with residents.

### 7. **Critique of PAP Candidates and Political Culture**
- Dr. Gomez accused the PAP of parachuting underperforming candidates into new constituencies and relying on self-praise rather than substance.
- He called for voters to reject such political culture and demand real accountability.

### 8. **Closing Rallying Call**
- Urged voters to give SDP a clear mandate (50.1% or more), to “sack” Ong Ye Kung and the PAP team in Sembawang.
- Emphasized that the power to change is in the hands of the voters, and that SDP candidates are ready to serve with passion, ideas, and accountability.

---

**In summary:**  
Dr. James Gomez’s speech was a strong call for Sembawang voters to reject the PAP’s fear tactics and lack of new ideas, and instead support the SDP’s vision for real change, accountability, and people-centered policies in housing, healthcare, and town management. He positioned the SDP as a credible, passionate alternative ready to serve and listen to residents, and urged voters to use their power to “reset” Sembawang by voting SDP on 3rd May.
