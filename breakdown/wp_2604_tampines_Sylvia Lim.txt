**Summary of Sylvia Lim's Speech at the Workers’ Party Rally, Tampines GRC, GE2025**

Sylvia Lim, Workers’ Party Chairperson and long-serving MP, delivered a speech focused on the needs and dignity of Singapore’s senior citizens. Here are the key points she made:

---

**1. Dedication to Seniors**
- Sylvia dedicated her speech to all senior citizens, both present at the rally and watching online, as well as to those who care for seniors in their families and communities.
- She emphasized that aging is inevitable for everyone, and the challenges seniors face today will eventually affect all Singaporeans.

**2. Workers’ Party Commitment**
- The Workers’ Party (WP) pledges to stand alongside seniors and fight for their interests in Parliament, recognizing their foundational role in building Singapore.

**3. Three Key Policy Proposals for Seniors**
- **a. Medisave Flexibility:**  
  - WP proposes that seniors over 60 should be allowed to use their Medisave to pay for all medical expenses not already covered by MediShield Life, Medifund, or other assistance schemes.
  - This should apply at government polyclinics, specialist outpatient clinics, and public sector CHAS clinics, especially for chronic diseases.
  - WP calls for the removal of current annual spending limits ($500 or $700), questioning why seniors cannot use their own Medisave balances for essential treatments.
- **b. Abolish Statutory Retirement Age:**  
  - WP calls for the removal of the statutory retirement age (currently 63).
  - Seniors should be allowed to work as long as they are able and willing, without an artificial ceiling.
  - She pointed out that many government ministers are already past this age, questioning the logic of the current policy.
- **c. Paid Family Care Leave for Caregivers:**  
  - WP proposes paid family care leave for those caring for elderly parents, similar to childcare leave.
  - The cost should be shared equally between employers and the government.
  - This signals that society values seniors being cared for at home by loved ones.

**4. Overarching Goal**
- WP’s goal is for seniors to live their golden years with security, dignity, and purpose.
- Sylvia stressed that Singapore should not be a place where seniors and their caregivers have to worry and make daily compromises just to survive.

**5. The Importance of Political Competition**
- She highlighted how increased opposition presence in Parliament has made the PAP pay more attention to citizens’ needs.
- Citing her own experience, she noted how the WP’s growing voice has led to more attention for issues affecting ordinary Singaporeans.

**6. Rallying Call**
- Sylvia urged Singaporeans, especially seniors, to send a strong signal at this election that more needs to be done for seniors.
- She called for voters to create a “silver tsunami” at the polls by voting for the Workers’ Party, to ensure seniors can live with security, dignity, and purpose.

---

**In summary:**  
Sylvia Lim’s speech was a heartfelt and policy-focused address on the needs of Singapore’s seniors, outlining concrete WP proposals to improve their healthcare, employment rights, and support for caregivers. She called on voters to support the Workers’ Party to ensure these changes can be realized, emphasizing the value of seniors and the importance of a balanced, competitive political system.
