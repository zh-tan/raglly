**Summary of Dr. Chee Soon Juan’s Speech at SDP Sembawang GRC Rally (Singapore Election 2025)**

Dr. Chee Soon Juan, candidate for Sembawang West SMC and Secretary-General of the Singapore Democratic Party (SDP), delivered a rousing speech focused on several key themes:

---

### 1. **PAP’s Pattern: “Criticize First, Copy Later”**
- Dr. Chee accused the ruling People’s Action Party (PAP) of a recurring tactic: **publicly criticizing SDP’s alternative policy proposals during elections, only to later adopt them without giving credit**.
- **Examples cited:**
  - **Unemployment insurance:** SDP proposed it; PAP called it “populist” in 2016, but later implemented a similar scheme under a different name.
  - **Higher income tax for top earners:** SDP proposed it to offset GST; PAP initially attacked the idea, then raised taxes for the top 5%.
  - **Minimum wage:** SDP proposed it in 2001; PAP rejected it, then introduced the Progressive Wage Model (a sectoral minimum wage) in 2012.
- Dr. Chee’s point: **SDP’s ideas have shaped national policy even without being in Parliament. Imagine what more could be done with SDP MPs elected.**

---

### 2. **SDP’s Vision: A Happier, Fairer Singapore**
- Dr. Chee contrasted the PAP’s focus on GDP and attracting foreign billionaires with SDP’s vision: **a Singapore where happiness, well-being, and security for all citizens are the main goals**.
- He cited Finland as an example of a small country that consistently ranks as the world’s happiest, due to fair opportunities, trust in government, and a strong social safety net.
- **SDP’s policy priorities:**
  - Expanding social safety nets
  - Universal healthcare
  - Education reform to nurture critical thinkers
  - Reducing inequality
  - Transparent and responsive governance

---

### 3. **Concrete Policy Proposals**
- **Healthcare:** SDP proposes a universal healthcare system to lower costs and prioritize care over bureaucracy.
- **Housing:** The “non-open market” HDB flat scheme—flats priced without land cost, sold back to HDB, making homes affordable for young Singaporeans.
- **Town Council Management:** SDP has a detailed plan for managing Sembawang Town Council, emphasizing accountability, robust financial systems, and community engagement.
- **Mental Health:** Making mental health a national priority, not an afterthought.

---

### 4. **Readiness and Capability of the SDP Team**
- Dr. Chee introduced the Sembawang GRC team, highlighting their professional backgrounds and readiness to manage the town council and serve residents.
- He emphasized the team’s **capability, compassion, and commitment**.

---

### 5. **Call to Action**
- Dr. Chee urged voters to **vote boldly for SDP** in Sembawang GRC, Sembawang West SMC, Bukit Panjang SMC, and Marsiling-Yew Tee GRC.
- He reassured voters that their vote is secret and encouraged them to cast their ballots with pride and hope.
- He promised that SDP MPs would **walk the ground, listen to residents, and put their well-being first**.

---

### 6. **Closing Message**
- Dr. Chee ended with a vision of a Singapore where **no one is left behind**, where policies are people-centered, and where the government is accountable to the people.
- He called on voters to **choose change, choose hope, and vote SDP** to build a better, more caring Singapore.

---

**In summary:**  
Dr. Chee Soon Juan’s speech was a passionate appeal for voters to reject the status quo, recognize SDP’s track record of policy innovation, and support a vision of Singapore that prioritizes happiness, fairness, and genuine care for all citizens. He positioned the SDP as a credible, ready, and compassionate alternative to the PAP, and called on voters to be bold in choosing change at the ballot box.
