### Gan Kim Yong’s Speech – Key Points

**Introduction and Gratitude**
- Gan Kim Yong introduced himself as a new member of the PAP Punggol GRC team, having joined just three days prior.
- He expressed his honor and happiness to be welcomed into the Punggol community.
- He thanked Senior Minister Teo Chee Hean for his decades of service to Punggol and Singapore, highlighting his guidance during the COVID-19 pandemic.

**Punggol’s Transformation and Community Spirit**
- Gan Kim Yong reflected on Punggol’s transformation from a rural area with kampongs to a vibrant, modern town.
- He recalled the ambitious plans for Punggol (Punggol 21 and Punggol 21 Plus) and how these visions have become reality in less than 20 years.
- He shared his observations from meeting residents:
    1. **Strong Multi-Generational Families:** He saw many grandparents actively involved in their grandchildren’s lives, indicating a cohesive and loving community.
    2. **Sense of Ownership:** Many residents have played a part in shaping Punggol, such as contributing to the development of the linear parks and the iconic waterway.
    3. **Imaginative and Integrated Design:** Punggol is designed for residents to live, work, and play together, with amenities like Coney Island, Waterway Point, and the upcoming Punggol Digital District.

**Listening and Responding to Residents’ Needs**
- Gan Kim Yong acknowledged the feedback he received from residents about areas for improvement, especially in:
    - **Connectivity:** There are currently 31 bus services, with new services and improvements made recently. The opening of Punggol Coast MRT and bus interchange will further enhance connectivity. More two-car LRT trains will be added to reduce congestion.
    - **Covered Walkways:** He recognized the need for more covered walkways due to Punggol’s green design and promised to work with relevant ministries to improve this.
    - **Community Spaces:** He noted requests for more spaces for exercise and socializing, promising to create more such opportunities.

**Upcoming and Ongoing Projects**
- **Upgrading of Punggol 21C:** Upgrading works will begin next year, with resident input.
- **New Punggol Digital Park Community Club:** To be built in the SIT compound by November 2026, serving both students and residents.
- **Punggol Coast Hawker Centre:** Opening in July 2025 to provide affordable meals.
- **Expansion of vending hubs and value meals:** To ensure affordable food options across Punggol.

**National and Economic Challenges**
- Gan Kim Yong addressed the global economic uncertainty, especially the new US tariffs affecting Singapore’s trade and economy.
- He explained that the Singapore Economic Resilience Task Force, which he chairs, is actively working to address these challenges, including negotiating with US counterparts.
- He reassured residents that the PAP government is prepared to support workers through upskilling, job support schemes, and will do more if necessary.
- He emphasized that while not every job can be saved, every worker will be supported.

**PAP’s Commitment and Call to Action**
- Gan Kim Yong highlighted the PAP team’s experience and dedication, with nearly 30 years of combined experience among the Punggol team, plus his own 24 years.
- He called for strong support from residents so the team can continue to serve and improve Punggol together.
- He concluded by emphasizing unity, resilience, and the importance of working together to overcome challenges and secure a better future for Punggol and Singapore.

Gan Kim Yong’s speech focused on Punggol’s remarkable transformation, the strong community spirit, and the PAP team’s commitment to listening and acting on residents’ needs. He addressed current economic challenges, outlined concrete plans for further improvements in connectivity, amenities, and affordability, and called on residents to support the PAP team to continue building a better Punggol and Singapore together.
