### Summary of Sun Xueling’s Speech at PAP Punggol GRC Rally

**Personal Connection and Empathy**
- Sun Xueling began her speech by sharing a touching encounter with a teenage girl who had been bullied and was moved by Sun’s own story of being bullied, which she had shared on Facebook. This encounter reinforced for Sun the importance of empathy, listening, and creating open channels for communication, especially regarding mental health and well-being.
- She recounted her initiative during COVID-19 called “Project It Will Be All Right,” which encouraged youths to share their mental health challenges and seek help, highlighting her commitment to mental wellness.

**Championing Young Families**
- Sun emphasized her focus on the needs of young families in Punggol. She listens closely to residents’ concerns, especially about the cost and challenges of raising children and balancing work and family.
- She cited her efforts in 2017 to address the high cost of infant milk formula, which led to a market study, a parliamentary motion, and eventually, the introduction of affordable house brands and tighter labeling rules.
- She has worked to expand preschool capacity and lower preschool costs. In the last five years, Punggol has seen the addition of about 520 infant care and 2,600 childcare places, with more to come in the next five years, including significant increases in Northshore.
- She also announced repeated decreases in childcare fees and new policies such as extended paternity leave and shared parental leave, as well as a pilot for infant child-minding, all aimed at supporting young parents.

**Track Record in Problem-Solving**
- Sun recounted her early days as MP for Punggol West, when she was pregnant and facing the challenge of 37 HDB blocks with faulty lifts. She described how she engaged residents, worked with ministries and contractors, and ultimately helped resolve the issue, leading to broader improvements in lift maintenance and technician training.
- She stressed that she not only listens to feedback but also takes concrete action, working with the civil service and stakeholders to implement solutions.

**Commitment to Residents and Town Council**
- Sun highlighted that DPM Gan Kim Yong intends to appoint her as the new Town Council Chairman for Punggol GRC if elected, citing her experience and dedication.
- She assured residents that they are “safe” with her and the PAP Punggol team, as they are committed to both listening and acting on residents’ concerns.

**Closing Message**
- Sun Xueling concluded by reaffirming the PAP’s identity as a party of action, not just words. She promised to continue championing the needs of families and residents, and asked for residents’ trust and support for the PAP team in Punggol.
