### Summary of Dr. Janil Puthucheary’s Speech

**1. Gratitude and Campaign Reflections**
- Dr. Janil opened by welcoming everyone, especially union members and the labor movement, and wished everyone a happy May Day.
- He reflected on the hard-fought campaign, highlighting the many interactions with residents who shared their stories, feedback, and concerns.
- He noted that residents are discerning, pay attention to candidates’ behavior, and appreciate the peaceful, polite nature of Singapore’s politics—even when candidates from different parties campaign side by side.

**2. Positive Campaigning and Real Plans**
- Dr. Janil emphasized that the PAP team in Punggol has not engaged in negative campaigning, personal attacks, or jokes at others’ expense.
- He stated that if a party does not resort to negativity, it must have “real plans”—and the PAP team does.

**3. Key Plans and Upcoming Improvements for Punggol**
- **Transport:** 
    - Introduction of two-car LRT cabins to improve service.
    - Opening of a new bus interchange at Punggol Coast MRT (as part of the Punggol Digital District) in June, with new bus services, especially for Northshore residents.
    - The old bus interchange will remain open and, in the long term, be converted into a permanent interchange connecting two MRT lines (Northeast Line and Cross Island Line).
    - Road network improvements, including a new bridge to Seletar Link to reduce congestion.
- **Sports and Recreation:**
    - Construction of a new sports stadium with 5,000 seats, five swimming pools, 20 badminton courts, gyms, fitness studios, and a water sports center, targeted to open in early 2026.
- **Active Aging and Seniors’ Support:**
    - Expansion of Active Aging Centres (AACs) in Punggol, including a new one at Punggol 21CC, to reduce social isolation and loneliness among seniors.
    - More senior citizens’ corners will be built in HDB estates to foster community and engagement.
    - These initiatives are a result of listening to residents, identifying needs, and working with partners and government agencies.

**4. Approach to Community Needs**
- Dr. Janil stressed the importance of understanding residents’ needs by “walking the ground,” listening, and engaging deeply.
- He described the PAP’s approach as one of identifying needs, finding solutions, bringing partners together, and lobbying government when necessary.
- He highlighted the responsibility of making balanced, well-considered plans that incorporate competing ideas and serve the community’s best interests.

**5. Closing Appeal**
- Dr. Janil concluded by reiterating that the PAP team has clear, actionable plans for Punggol, developed from a deep understanding of residents’ aspirations.
- He called on residents to support the PAP to realize these plans and make Punggol a better home for all.
