### Summary of Yeo Wan Ling’s Speech at the PAP Punggol GRC Rally

**Yeo Wan Ling** began by greeting Punggol residents, union sisters and brothers, and PAP supporters, noting the significance of the day as both the last day of campaigning for GE2025 and May Day, a day to celebrate workers.

She reflected on the heartfelt moments during the campaign, expressing gratitude for the support from volunteers, activists, unionists, and residents. She shared how Punggol and its people have become very special to her over the past five years, motivating her to continue serving.

A key moment she recounted was a conversation with a young couple at Waterway Point, where they asked her, “Why should we vote for PAP?” She responded that a good vote requires both rational thought and gut feel, and that it is important to choose representatives who are kind, hardworking, and able to rally partnerships to bring progress and happiness to the community.

Yeo Wan Ling emphasized her deep connection to Punggol, saying every conversation with residents about their hopes, concerns, and dreams for their children drives her to work harder and dream up new projects for the community.

#### Focus on Jobs and Workers

- She highlighted her commitment to workers, saying jobs are not just numbers but represent livelihoods and the future of Punggol families.
- She praised Punggol’s youth for their aspirations and volunteer spirit, and stressed the importance of providing them with opportunities for growth, especially amid global uncertainties.
- Since 2020, the PAP Punggol team has partnered with NTUC’s e2i to support employment and employability for residents of all ages and backgrounds.
    - In 2024, over 800 residents engaged with career support programs, and 180 were successfully placed in jobs through these efforts.
    - In March 2025, Punggol launched its flagship career marketplace, offering over 1,000 job vacancies, with 10% targeted at fresh graduates.
- She announced plans to expand the career marketplace from twice a year to every two months, and to launch new workshops in partnership with e2i focusing on in-demand skills, including AI and the digital economy.
- Yeo addressed concerns from mid-career professionals seeking career changes, introducing a new partnership with the Singapore Institute of Technology for a lifestyle entrepreneurship program, which will help residents start micro-businesses, supported by seed funding from the Punggol Shore Merchants Association.

#### Support for Families and Caregivers

- Recognizing the pressures faced by young families, especially mothers who want to return to work but have caregiving duties, she pledged to continue advocating for flexible work arrangements (FWAs) in Parliament.
- She announced the extension of the NTUC “See You Back at Work” program to include micro-jobs in Punggol, such as babysitting, healthcare assistance, tuition, and survey work, in partnership with Project Success.

#### Closing

Yeo Wan Ling concluded by urging residents to consider whether they want to build on the strong foundations already laid, ensure access to good jobs and meaningful careers, and support a team with a proven track record of delivering results for workers and families. She called on residents to vote for the PAP, emphasizing that behind every job is a family, behind every paycheck is dignity, and behind every skills upgrade is hope for a better tomorrow.
