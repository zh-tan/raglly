**Summary of Siti Alia Binte Abdul Rahim Mattar’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Punggol GRC Rally (GE2025):**

Siti Alia Binte Abdul Rahim Mattar, candidate for Punggol GRC, introduced herself as a parent and a former legal counsel at the Monetary Authority of Singapore, now working in the financial industry. She emphasized that while Punggol may be new to her team, the concerns and aspirations of its residents are familiar and shared by many Singaporeans.

**Key Points from Her Speech:**

1. **Understanding Residents’ Concerns:**
   - She highlighted that, like many residents, she juggles parenting and caring for elderly parents.
   - Residents have voiced concerns about transport issues, internet connectivity, noise from jets, rising food prices, and especially the increasing cost and stress of raising children, including those with special needs.
   - She recounted meeting a caregiver whose household income disqualified her from subsidies, yet the costs of caring for a child with cerebral palsy were overwhelming. Alia stressed that both children and caregivers need support.

2. **Seniors and Healthcare:**
   - Many seniors are anxious about rising medical costs, lack of steady income, and having to pay GST on medication, even for chronic conditions.
   - She questioned why Singaporeans must struggle to use their own CPF and Medisave savings for healthcare, emphasizing that this is not asking for handouts but simply wanting to care for themselves with their own money.

3. **Call for Transparency and Inclusion:**
   - Alia criticized the government’s tendency to make decisions “behind closed doors” and the culture of relying on personal connections for national programs.
   - She called for greater transparency and stakeholder involvement in national decisions, arguing that Singaporeans are the true stakeholders and should be included in decisions made in their name.

4. **Workers’ Party Approach:**
   - She asserted that the Workers’ Party is transparent, open, and bases its policies on what Singaporeans want.
   - The party values Singapore’s diversity and believes that what unites Singaporeans is more important than what divides them.

5. **Call to Action:**
   - Alia urged Punggol voters that it is time for change and to vote for the Workers’ Party, which she described as “working for Singapore.”
   - She concluded with a brief message in Malay, reinforcing the importance of national-level plans and representation in Parliament.

**Overall Message:**
Siti Alia positioned herself and the Workers’ Party as empathetic, transparent, and committed to addressing real concerns faced by ordinary Singaporeans—especially families, caregivers, and seniors. She called for more openness in governance and greater involvement of citizens in decision-making, urging voters to support the Workers’ Party for a more inclusive and responsive Singapore.
