**Summary of Pritam Singh's Speech at the Workers' Party Rally (Hougang, GE2025):**

Pritam Singh, the Secretary-General of the Workers’ Party (WP) and Leader of the Opposition, delivered a key speech at the final WP rally for GE2025 in Hougang. His address focused on countering several arguments made by the ruling People’s Action Party (PAP) during the campaign, providing what he called “reality checks” for voters.

**Key Points from Pritam Singh’s Speech:**

1. **On the PAP Needing a Strong Mandate for International Negotiations:**
   - The PAP claims that a strong mandate (meaning near-total parliamentary dominance) is necessary for Singapore to be respected internationally.
   - Pritam counters that even if WP wins every seat it contests (which is highly unlikely), the PAP would still hold 71 out of 97 seats (73%), a supermajority by any international standard.
   - He argues that foreign leaders from democratic countries would envy such dominance and would not see Singapore as weak.

2. **On More Opposition Weakening the Government:**
   - The PAP says more opposition MPs would make it harder to govern.
   - Pritam points out that with a majority, the PAP can pass any law or constitutional amendment it wants. Historically, the PAP has always had more than enough MPs to govern effectively.
   - He emphasizes that WP does not oppose for the sake of opposing, but supports good policies and only challenges those that need improvement.

3. **On the Need for Ministers in Crisis:**
   - The PAP warns that not electing key ministers (like DPM Gan Kim Yong) would endanger Singapore in a crisis.
   - Pritam highlights the contradiction: the PAP itself moved DPM Gan to contest in a new GRC, risking his seat, and allowed four ministers to retire. If these ministers were truly indispensable, the PAP would not have taken such risks.
   - He notes that even if ministers lose elections, they can still serve Singapore in other capacities, as is common in other countries.

4. **On the Opposition Being “Here to Stay”:**
   - PM Lawrence Wong claims the opposition is now a permanent feature.
   - Pritam warns voters not to be complacent—there are no “safe seats” for the opposition, and every vote is crucial. The PAP always campaigns hard to win every seat.

5. **On “Free Passes” for the Opposition:**
   - PM Wong says voters should hold opposition candidates to the same standards as PAP candidates.
   - Pritam argues that the GRC system gives PAP candidates “free passes” by allowing new or unknown candidates to ride on the coattails of ministers, while opposition candidates face much higher risks and personal sacrifices.
   - He asserts that WP candidates are brave and resilient, standing for a more balanced political system.

6. **On the Role of the Workers’ Party:**
   - Pritam stresses that the WP is a “force for good,” providing necessary checks and balances in Parliament.
   - He points out that WP MPs have contributed concrete policy proposals and constructive debate, not just “talk.”
   - He criticizes the PAP for not engaging substantively on key issues like housing affordability and GST hikes during the campaign.

7. **Call to Action:**
   - Pritam urges voters to make their votes count, not to be swayed by fear, and to support the WP for a more balanced and participatory democracy.
   - He reassures voters that the PAP will remain the government even if WP wins all the seats it contests, and that a stronger opposition will only make Singapore’s system more robust.

**Conclusion:**
Pritam Singh’s speech was a direct rebuttal to the PAP’s campaign messaging, aiming to dispel fears about voting for the opposition and to encourage Singaporeans to vote for the Workers’ Party as a responsible, rational, and necessary check on the government. He emphasized the importance of a balanced parliament and the value of courageous, independent voices in Singapore’s political system.
