### Summary of Pritam Singh’s Speech

**Opening and Vision**
- Pritam Singh, as Leader of the Opposition and WP Secretary-General, addressed residents of Tampines GRC and Tampines Changi SMC, emphasizing the Workers’ Party’s commitment to always work for Singapore.
- He highlighted that the WP manifesto is filled with practical, specific proposals that appeal to both the heart and head, and are meant for all Singaporeans regardless of race, language, or religion.

**Critique of the PAP Manifesto**
- Singh criticized the PAP’s 2025 manifesto as being “short on substance and specifics” and “loaded with motherhood statements.”
- He argued that Singapore is now a much more complex society than in 1965, and thus needs fresh thinking and concrete policy ideas, not vague promises.
- He accused the PAP of asking Singaporeans to “sign a blank cheque” by not providing details, especially on critical issues like housing affordability and HDB lease decay.
- He pointed out that the PAP’s only solution to housing affordability seems to be increasing taxpayer subsidies, without explaining where the money will come from.
- On the issue of HDB lease decay and the Voluntary Early Redevelopment Scheme (VERS), Singh noted that the PAP has not provided enough details for families to plan for their future.

**The Need for Checks and Balances**
- Singh stressed the importance of a balanced parliament, not a “rubber stamp,” so that policies are properly debated and scrutinized.
- He argued that a vote for the Workers’ Party is a vote to ensure Singaporeans’ voices are heard in Parliament.

**On PAP-NTUC Candidates**
- Singh highlighted that two PAP candidates in single-member constituencies (SMCs) are top leaders in NTUC (the national trade union).
- He questioned the need for more PAP-NTUC MPs in Parliament, noting that NTUC is already closely linked to the PAP and that losing PAP candidates often find “safe harbor” jobs in NTUC.
- He cited the example of the NTUC Income sale to Allianz, where only WP MPs raised questions in Parliament, while PAP labor MPs were silent.

**Call to Action**
- Singh urged voters to support WP candidates Kenneth Foo (Tampines Changi SMC) and Andre Loh (Jalan Kayu SMC), arguing that electing more WP MPs will make a real difference and strengthen checks and balances.
- He emphasized that Parliament should be a place for rational, responsible, and respectable debate, and that the WP’s only “symbiotic relationship” is with the people of Singapore.

**On Foreign Influence**
- Singh categorically rejected any foreign involvement in Singapore’s domestic politics, stating that “Singapore is our business, nobody else’s.”
- He stressed that the Workers’ Party only needs the support of Singaporeans, not any foreign elements.

**Personal Commitment**
- Singh shared his personal motivation for entering politics: to build a more just and fair Singapore, especially for minorities, and to advance a race-neutral society where people are judged by their deeds and common humanity.

**Closing**
- He ended with the WP rallying cry, “Majulah Singapura,” and wished everyone a safe journey home, reminding them to keep the venue clean.

**Key Themes from Pritam Singh’s Speech:**
- The need for detailed, practical policies (contrasting WP’s manifesto with PAP’s).
- The importance of checks and balances in Parliament.
- Critique of the PAP’s dominance and lack of transparency.
- The value of a loyal opposition that works for all Singaporeans.
- Rejection of foreign interference in Singapore politics.
- Personal and party commitment to a fairer, more inclusive Singapore.

**In summary:**  
Pritam Singh called on Singaporeans to vote for the Workers’ Party to ensure a more balanced, accountable, and inclusive government, with real debate and practical solutions to Singapore’s challenges. He positioned the WP as a loyal opposition, committed to Singapore and its people, and contrasted this with the PAP’s lack of specifics and over-reliance on the civil service.
