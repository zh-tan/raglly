### Lim Swee Say's Key Points at the PAP Punggol GRC Rally

**1. Importance of Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)**
- Lim Swee Say emphasized that attracting foreign investment is crucial for Singapore’s continued access to advanced technology and global markets.
- He shared an anecdote from his time at EDB (Economic Development Board) about how global companies choose where to invest, highlighting three key criteria (“three T’s”):
    - **Capability:** Can the country do the work? Singapore scores well here.
    - **Capacity:** Does the country have the resources (land, water, manpower) to support and grow the investment? Singapore also scores well.
    - **Policy and Political Stability:** Is there long-term stability in government and policy? This is the most important factor for major, long-term investments. Singapore is ranked number one among top choices because of its stability.

**2. Political Stability and the Stakes of the Election**
- Lim stressed that Singapore’s political and policy stability is a major reason why top companies invest here.
- He warned that if the PAP government and the new 4G (fourth generation) leadership under Prime Minister Lawrence Wong do not receive a strong mandate in this election, Singapore’s reputation for stability could be undermined.
- This could lead to global companies questioning Singapore’s future stability, potentially resulting in fewer investments, fewer jobs, and a less secure future for Singaporeans.

**3. The Significance of This Election**
- Lim Swee Say argued that this election is not just about PAP versus Workers’ Party or other opposition parties.
- The outcome will determine the strength and stability of Singapore’s leadership for the next 10, 15, or even 20 years.
- A strong mandate for the 4G leadership is necessary to ensure continued success and to nurture the next (5G) generation of leaders.

**4. Call to Action**
- He urged voters to reflect carefully during Cooling-Off Day about what is at stake.
- Lim appealed to Singaporeans to make the “right choice” on polling day, to ensure Singapore can continue to move forward together with strong, stable leadership.
