### Summary of Dennis Tan Lip Fong’s Speech (Hougang SMC, WP Rally GE2025)

**Introduction and Gratitude**
- Dennis Tan began by greeting the residents of Hougang SMC and Singaporeans, expressing his honor and privilege to have served Hougang for the past five years.

- Dennis emphasized his efforts to be present on the ground every day, making himself available to both HDB and condo residents, and working closely with management councils.

- He highlighted how residents’ feedback on key issues—cost of living, healthcare, employment, housing, and more—has enabled him to represent their concerns in Parliament.

- Dennis detailed the ongoing upgrading and improvement projects in Hougang, including neighborhood renewal, repainting, home improvement, new playgrounds, fitness stations, and extended sheltered walkways.

**The Importance of Alternative Voices**
- He reminded residents that Hougang has been a beacon of democracy since 1991, understanding the need for alternative voices in Parliament.
- Dennis argued that having one more PAP MP would not make a difference, but more WP MPs would provide better scrutiny and accountability.
- He pointed out that WP MPs, including himself, voted against the GST increase, which contributed to the rising cost of living, while PAP MPs supported it.

**Call for More Diverse Representation**
- Dennis stressed the importance of having more heads and different ideas in Parliament, especially in uncertain global times, rather than everyone “singing the same song.”
- He noted that WP MPs were outnumbered 1 to 9 in the last Parliament, and called for more WP MPs to better scrutinize the government.

**Assurance and Closing**
- He reassured residents that he and the WP are privileged to serve Hougang and hope to continue doing so with strong support.
- Dennis concluded by urging residents to remember the impact of their vote, especially regarding the GST and cost of living, and to vote for the Workers’ Party on May 3rd to keep the government on its toes and ensure a better future for all Singaporeans.
