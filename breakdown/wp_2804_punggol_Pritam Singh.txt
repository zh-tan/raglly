**Summary of Pritam Singh’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Punggol GRC Rally (GE2025):**

Pritam Singh, the Secretary-General of the Workers’ Party (WP), delivered a passionate speech focusing on the **rising cost of living** and the **PAP government’s decision to raise the GST (Goods and Services Tax) during a period of high inflation**. He criticized the PAP for being the only government in the world to “turbocharge” inflation by increasing GST when global and local prices were already surging, arguing that this move caused unnecessary pain to Singaporeans, especially seniors and those on fixed incomes.

He pointed out that the government had a **$14 billion surplus** in the last parliamentary term, questioning why the GST hike could not have been postponed to give Singaporeans some breathing space. He cited the Monetary Authority of Singapore’s own statement that a GST hike could increase inflation by up to 40%, directly contradicting the PAP’s claims that the timing was not problematic.

Pritam argued that the **PAP gets away with such unpopular decisions because there is not enough political pressure from opposition MPs**. He urged voters to elect more WP MPs, explaining that the PAP cares deeply about its vote share and number of seats—these are their “KPIs.” By voting for the WP, Singaporeans can send a strong message to the PAP to reconsider or delay unpopular policies like further GST hikes.

He emphasized that **WP MPs in Parliament ask the tough questions and propose alternative policies**, forcing the PAP to justify its decisions and sometimes adopt WP’s ideas years later. He stressed that the WP is not naive or populist, but does its homework and understands the need for prudent fiscal management. However, he insisted that decisions about how to fund future spending should not be left to the PAP alone, but should involve all Singaporeans through robust parliamentary debate.

Pritam also addressed the **issue of reserves**, noting that Singapore has accumulated massive investment returns (NIRC) in recent years, with much of it locked away and not used to help Singaporeans today. He questioned whether the government is overburdening the current generation in the name of saving for the future, especially when CPF payouts are not inflation-indexed and housing remains unaffordable for many.

On the **PAP’s candidate for Punggol GRC, DPM Gan Kim Yong**, Pritam questioned his commitment, noting that Gan was not elected into the PAP’s central executive committee and may only serve one term. He argued that government funding for local projects should not depend on personal relationships with the Prime Minister, but should be allocated fairly to all Singaporeans.

Pritam reminded voters that **opposition MPs are necessary to hold the government accountable**, citing examples where PAP MPs have made questionable proposals (like university degrees expiring after five years) due to overconfidence from their supermajority. He highlighted that only opposition MPs will challenge and push the PAP to listen and change course when public support is strong.

He concluded by urging Punggol voters to seize this historic opportunity to send a strong message to the PAP, not just for their own constituency but for all Singaporeans. He warned that the PAP’s practice of shifting electoral boundaries could undermine opposition gains elsewhere, but Punggol’s boundaries are less easily changed. By voting WP, Punggol residents can help ensure a more balanced parliament, fairer policies, and a government that truly listens to the people.

**Key Points from Pritam Singh:**
- The PAP raised GST at the worst possible time, worsening inflation and cost of living.
- The government had enough surplus to avoid the GST hike.
- Only the PAP, among world governments, raised consumption taxes during raging inflation.
- The PAP can make such decisions because there is not enough opposition in Parliament.
- Voting WP increases political pressure, forcing the PAP to reconsider unpopular policies.
- WP MPs ask tough questions and propose alternatives, leading to better outcomes for Singaporeans.
- Decisions about reserves and spending should involve all Singaporeans, not just the PAP.
- Government funding for local projects should be fair, not based on political connections.
- Only opposition MPs will hold the government accountable and prevent policy complacency.
- Punggol voters have a unique opportunity to send a strong message for a more balanced and just Singapore.

**Closing Call:**  
Pritam Singh called on voters to support the Workers’ Party for a fairer, more accountable, and more responsive government—one that truly works for all Singaporeans.
