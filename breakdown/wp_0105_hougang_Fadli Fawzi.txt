**Summary of Fadli Fawzi’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Rally (GE2025, Hougang/Aljunied GRC):**

Fadli Fawzi, candidate for Aljunied GRC, delivered a rousing speech focused on the central theme of **courage and hope versus fear** in Singapore’s politics. Here are the key points he made:

1. **Choice at the Ballot Box:**  
   - In two days, Singaporeans will decide who represents them in Parliament and what kind of future they want for Singapore.

2. **PAP’s Politics of Fear:**  
   - Fadli argued that the PAP is “selling fear” to voters: fear that Singapore cannot survive without them, fear that opposition wards will become slums, fear that alternative voices will destabilize government, and fear of being labeled “free riders.”
   - He challenged the audience: “Do you want to be afraid?”

3. **Workers’ Party’s Politics of Hope and Courage:**  
   - Contrasting with the PAP, Fadli said the Workers’ Party stands for hope—“not blind, desperate, or reckless hope, but careful hope born of experience, hard work, and resilience.”
   - He emphasized the need for courage: courage to keep fighting for Singapore, to have faith in ourselves, to face our fears, and to lead by example.
   - He called for a Singapore where everyone can flourish, not just the wealthy, and where there is greater accountability and transparency in government.

4. **What Singaporeans Should Really Fear:**  
   - If there is any fear, it should be of being priced out of our own homeland, of stagnation, or of leaders being out of touch.
   - He rejected the idea that WP-run estates become slums, citing his own lifelong residence in Aljunied and the repeated re-election of WP MPs.

5. **Voting for WP is an Act of Courage and Hope:**  
   - Fadli reminded voters that their vote is secret and that almost 1 million Singaporeans voted for the opposition in the last election.
   - He reassured that the government cannot monitor everyone’s vote.

6. **WP’s Track Record:**  
   - WP constituencies have not become slums; instead, residents are well-served.
   - Voting for WP is not being a “free rider”—it is playing a vital role in holding the government accountable.

7. **Salute to Supporters:**  
   - He thanked supporters for their dedication, braving mud, rain, and long hours to support the party.

8. **Final Rallying Call:**  
   - He urged voters not to let fear govern their vote, but to “step up with courage, vote for hope, vote for the Workers’ Party.”

**In summary:**  
Fadli Fawzi’s speech was a passionate call for Singaporeans to reject fear-based politics and instead choose courage and hope by voting for the Workers’ Party. He emphasized the importance of alternative voices, accountability, and the belief that Singapore can do better with a more balanced and democratic system.
