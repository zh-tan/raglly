### Summary of Muhammad Faizal Bin Abdul Manap’s Speech

**1. Addressing Voters’ Concerns**
- Faizal began by acknowledging that many Tampines GRC voters were still undecided and had questions about municipal matters, ongoing projects, grassroots activities, and vote secrecy.
- He aimed to clarify these issues to help voters make an informed decision.

**2. Municipal and Town Council Management**
- Faizal assured residents that if WP is elected, they would review the existing town plans shared by the incumbent PAP team. If the plans are suitable, they will proceed; if not, they may tweak or change them after consulting residents.
- He emphasized WP’s experience and expertise in running town councils, citing WP’s management of Hougang (34 years), Aljunied (14 years), and Sengkang (5 years), all of which have received top ratings for estate cleanliness and management from the Ministry of National Development.

**3. Continuity of Government Projects**
- Faizal clarified that projects managed by government agencies (like LTA, HDB, NParks) will continue regardless of which party manages the town, as these are public agencies serving all Singaporeans.

**4. Grassroots and Community Activities**
- He addressed concerns that opposition wards might lose out on grassroots activities if WP is elected. Faizal argued the opposite: in WP-held wards, residents benefit from three sources of support—the WP MP, the People’s Association (PA), and the PAP branch—leading to more events and assistance than in PAP-only wards.
- He gave the example of his own ward, Kaki Bukit, where residents enjoy double or even triple the number of festive events and support schemes.

**5. Vote Secrecy**
- Faizal strongly reassured voters that their votes are secret and there is no need to fear repercussions for voting for the opposition. He cited the long history of WP-held wards like Hougang and Aljunied, where residents have never been penalized for their vote.

**6. Encouragement to Vote with Heart and Courage**
- He advised undecided voters to “close your eyes and focus on your heart” when marking the ballot, emphasizing that the pen or stamp is “mightier than the sword” and that their vote will shape the future for themselves and future generations.

**7. Personal Touch and Closing**
- Faizal humorously shared that the warmth and support from residents during the campaign had helped him lose weight and become healthier.
- He ended by urging Tampines GRC residents to keep him and his team “healthy” by voting for them, promising to work hard for the next five years as their elected representatives.

**8. Core Message**
- Vote for the Workers’ Party for experienced, responsible town management, more community support, and a stronger, more balanced voice in Parliament.
- Don’t be afraid—vote with hope and conviction for a better Singapore.
