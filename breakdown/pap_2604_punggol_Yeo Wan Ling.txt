**Summary of Yeo Wan Ling’s Speech at the PAP Punggol GRC Rally (GE2025):**

Yeo Wan Ling, the PAP candidate for Punggol GRC, addressed residents, union members, and supporters with a heartfelt and personal speech. She reflected on her journey with the Punggol community, emphasizing the strong bonds and trust built over the years through shared experiences, from casual coffees to late-night emails and home visits.

**Key Points from Yeo Wan Ling’s Speech:**

1. **Commitment to All Generations:**
   - Yeo highlighted that Punggol is not just a young town, but home to many multi-generational families who have lived there for over 20 years.
   - She stressed the importance of supporting seniors, noting initiatives like the launch of a second free TCM clinic and the establishment of active aging hubs and centers.
   - She announced the resumption of popular community programs such as the kopi corners, which foster social interaction among seniors.

2. **Support for Families and Workers:**
   - Yeo shared real stories of Punggol residents facing challenges—single parents, retrenched workers, caregivers, and women returning to the workforce.
   - She explained how these stories inspired her advocacy in Parliament for flexible work arrangements, the progressive wage model, and landmark workplace fairness legislation to protect women and caregivers from discrimination.
   - She has championed better protections for platform workers (e.g., food delivery riders, private hire drivers), ensuring fair compensation and CPF contributions.

3. **Local Employment and Empowerment Initiatives:**
   - Through NTUC and local partnerships, Yeo has expanded programs like “See You Back at Work” for mothers and caregivers, career fairs, mentorships, and the “Job Container” initiative, which has helped over 8,000 residents find employment.
   - She introduced upcoming programs to support mid-career entrepreneurship.

4. **Focus on Youth and Mental Wellness:**
   - Yeo recognized Punggol’s large youth population and described efforts to address their concerns about future jobs and mental health.
   - She highlighted community-driven initiatives like the “Positivity Pizza Movement” to combat cyberbullying and promote mental wellness.
   - She is committed to creating more safe, engaging spaces and programs for teenagers and families.

5. **Personal Approach and Advocacy:**
   - Yeo emphasized that her work is driven by the real-life experiences and needs of Punggol residents, which she brings to Parliament.
   - She promised to remain a friend and advocate, not just during elections but every day, listening and acting on residents’ concerns.

6. **Call to Action:**
   - She asked residents for their mandate to continue serving as their voice in Parliament, pledging honesty, clarity, and courage in representing Punggol’s interests.
   - She concluded by urging residents to vote for the PAP, reaffirming her commitment to walk alongside them and build a better Punggol together.

**In summary:**  
Yeo Wan Ling’s speech was deeply personal and focused on her track record of action for seniors, families, workers, and youth in Punggol. She highlighted concrete initiatives, her advocacy in Parliament, and her ongoing commitment to serve and improve the lives of all residents, calling on voters to support the PAP team for continued progress.
