**Summary of Alexis Dang Pei Yuan’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Punggol GRC Rally (GE2025):**

Alexis Dang Pei Yuan, one of the Workers’ Party (WP) candidates for Punggol GRC, delivered a speech that focused on several key themes:

1. **Addressing Comments from the Ruling Party:**
   - Alexis responded to remarks made by PAP speakers, particularly the statement that “we do not need strangers to come to Punggol.” She questioned whether this applied to DPM Gan Kim Yong, who was parachuted into Punggol, or if he was “exempted” due to his connections with PM Lawrence Wong.
   - She criticized the political culture where success is based on connections rather than fairness and merit.

2. **Call for Political Fairness and Balanced Parliament:**
   - Alexis argued against the idea that only the ruling party can deliver results, pointing out that Singaporeans deserve a more balanced parliament and credible opposition.
   - She highlighted the PAP’s tactic of parachuting key figures into constituencies to force voters into difficult choices, referencing the 2020 East Coast GRC example and drawing parallels to the current situation in Punggol.

3. **Appeal to Voters:**
   - Alexis urged Punggol residents to reject the use of high-profile government figures as political pawns.
   - She called on voters to say “no” to being forced into such choices and to demand better political standards and progress.
   - She emphasized that the WP team in Punggol is a “solid, solid team” and that they have stepped up to serve residents.

4. **Personal Connection and Commitment:**
   - Alexis shared that before entering politics, she was an ordinary citizen and has deep respect for Singapore’s leaders, but she is troubled by the current political culture.
   - She promised that if given the chance, the WP team would act immediately on residents’ concerns and work for all Singaporeans.

5. **Closing Rallying Call:**
   - She encouraged Punggol residents to step up for all Singaporeans by voting for the Workers’ Party, emphasizing that they deserve better and that the WP is ready to serve.

**In summary:**  
Alexis Dang’s speech was a strong rebuttal to PAP’s rhetoric about “strangers” and parachuted candidates, a call for political fairness, and a passionate appeal for voters to support a credible opposition for a more balanced and progressive Singapore. She positioned the WP Punggol team as committed, relatable, and ready to serve, urging residents to vote for change and not fall for old political tactics.
