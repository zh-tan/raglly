### Summary of Sun Xueling’s Speech at PAP Punggol GRC Rally

**Sun Xueling** focused her speech on two main topics important to Punggol families and children: **transport issues** and **support for families and children’s development**.

#### 1. **Transport and Connectivity**
- She acknowledged that many Punggol residents spend significant time commuting for work, school, and family needs, which takes away valuable time from loved ones.
- Sun shared her personal experience as a student, spending 1.5 hours commuting to school, and how exhausting and draining it was.
- She emphasized that transport issues affect both physical and mental well-being and assured residents that she and her team are aware of these concerns, having engaged with the community regularly over the past five years.
- She highlighted the efforts to improve transport, such as the introduction of the two-car LRT carriages (ordered in 2022, arriving in 2024, and rolling out in July 2025), and ongoing construction of roads and MRT lines in Punggol.
- Sun stressed that these developments show Punggol is constantly improving, increasing the value and convenience of the estate for residents.

#### 2. **Community Initiatives for Children and Families**
- When Sun first arrived in Punggol West 10 years ago, there were few facilities for young families and children—not even a library.
- She initiated a **container library** in 2016 to give children early access to books and learning, starting with 3,500 books.
- As the community grew and the Punggol Regional Library opened, she upgraded the container library into **The Lighthouse at Punggol**—a safe space for children and youth to receive free academic support, enrichment classes, and access to mentors and counselors.
- Sun shared touching stories of children and families who benefited from these initiatives, such as:
    - A teenager with social anxiety who gained confidence through drama classes.
    - An 11-year-old boy overcoming trauma and negative self-image with counseling.
    - A young boy whose family received furniture support and academic help, allowing him and his siblings to thrive.
- She emphasized that these programs are carefully curated with input from experts (e.g., the Dean of NUS Medicine, CEO of IMH) to address real needs in the community.

#### 3. **Future Developments and Opportunities**
- Sun updated residents on upcoming educational facilities: a new primary school, secondary school, and junior college in Punggol, as well as the new Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) campus, which will accommodate 11,000 undergraduates.
- She assured parents and children that opportunities are being brought closer to home, so they do not need to travel far for quality education and enrichment.

#### 4. **Commitment to Action**
- Sun reiterated that the PAP is a party of action, not just words, and that she and her team are dedicated to galvanizing community resources to help every child and family in Punggol thrive.
- She concluded by expressing optimism for the future of Punggol’s children and youth, stating that the best chapters are still being written, and encouraged residents to continue supporting the PAP for ongoing progress.

Sun Xueling’s speech was heartfelt and focused on practical improvements in transport and community support for families and children in Punggol. She highlighted her track record of initiating ground-up solutions, her personal connection to residents’ struggles, and her commitment to making Punggol a better, more supportive place for all. She urged residents to look forward to more opportunities and to vote for the PAP to continue this work.
