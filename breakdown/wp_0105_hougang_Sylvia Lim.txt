**Summary of Sylvia Lim’s Speech at the Workers’ Party GE2025 Rally (Hougang GRC):**

Sylvia Lim, a veteran Workers’ Party (WP) MP and candidate for Aljunied GRC, delivered a speech focused on hope, renewal, and the importance of a balanced political system in Singapore. Here are the key points from her address:

1. **Gratitude and Reflection:**
   - Sylvia began by thanking the police and security personnel for ensuring the rallies and elections proceeded smoothly.
   - She reflected on her own journey with Aljunied residents, recalling her first contest in 2006, her entry into Parliament as a Non-Constituency MP, and the WP’s historic win in Aljunied GRC in 2011.

2. **Challenging the Politics of Fear:**
   - She recounted how, in 2011, the PAP warned voters that losing ministers in Aljunied would weaken Singapore, even saying residents would have “five years to repent” if they voted WP.
   - Sylvia pointed out that despite these warnings, life went on, Singapore was not weakened, and the WP team delivered on its promises.

3. **Track Record and Achievements:**
   - Over the past 14 years, WP MPs in Aljunied, Hougang, and Sengkang filed over 2,700 parliamentary questions, raised motions on key issues (cost of living, housing, healthcare, women’s empowerment, etc.), and worked hard to improve estate facilities.
   - She emphasized the WP’s commitment to both parliamentary work and estate management, highlighting improvements and five-year plans for HDB residents.

4. **Personal Connection and Renewal:**
   - Sylvia shared her appreciation for the support and care shown by residents, mentioning personal gestures like congratulatory cards for her recent marriage.
   - She introduced new candidates in Aljunied—Fadli Fawzi and Kenneth Tong—stressing the importance of renewal for both the party and Singapore’s future.

5. **Rejecting Fear, Voting with Hope:**
   - She urged voters not to be swayed by fear-mongering or warnings about losing ministers, but to vote with conviction and hope for a better future.
   - Sylvia called for a promise to vote with hope, not fear, and to stand up for Singapore by supporting the Workers’ Party.

6. **Passing the Baton:**
   - She concluded by expressing the wish that, years from now, Singaporeans can look back with pride at having stood up for their country and helped shape its future.
   - Sylvia ended with a call to “wake up on Sunday morning to clear blue skies” and to vote for the Workers’ Party, “working for Singapore.”

**In summary:**  
Sylvia Lim’s speech was a rallying call for voters to reject the politics of fear, recognize the WP’s proven track record, embrace renewal, and vote with hope and conviction for a more balanced and democratic Singapore.
