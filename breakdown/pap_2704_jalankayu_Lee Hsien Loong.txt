## **Summary of Lee Hsien Loong’s Speech at Jalan Kayu SMC Rally (GE2025)**

**1. Four Key Points of the Election**
- Lee Hsien Loong began by outlining four main points:
    1. What this election is about.
    2. The global problems affecting Singapore.
    3. The need for a strong and good government to handle these challenges.
    4. What the PAP can do for Singaporeans.

**2. The World Has Changed – Challenges Ahead**
- He emphasized that the world is now more uncertain, with trade wars, tariffs, and unpredictable changes.
- These global issues make it harder for Singaporeans to make a living, for companies to export, and for people to plan for the future.
- He warned that these problems are not temporary; Singapore must be psychologically prepared for a tougher environment.

**3. PAP’s Response and Support for Singaporeans**
- The government anticipated these challenges and prepared a large support package in the latest Budget, including CDC vouchers, GST vouchers, SG60 vouchers, and more.
- A typical family could receive up to $5,000 in government support this year.
- There are also job support and retraining initiatives to help Singaporeans keep or find new jobs.
- Lee contrasted PAP’s tangible actions with the opposition’s focus on lengthy manifestos, arguing that PAP’s track record speaks for itself.

**4. Importance of Good Government and Strong Team**
- Lee stressed that Singapore’s success during crises like COVID-19 was due to having a competent, united government and team.
- He warned that every GRC or seat lost means losing ministers and capable leaders, which weakens the government’s ability to respond to crises.
- He specifically highlighted the importance of keeping key ministers like Heng Swee Keat and Gan Kim Yong in Parliament, as they are responsible for critical portfolios like the economy and crisis management.

**5. On Opposition’s Role and Claims**
- Lee addressed opposition claims that more opposition MPs are needed to ask tough questions.
- He pointed out that PAP MPs do ask questions and scrutinize policies, citing the recent Income-Allianz deal as an example where PAP MPs raised concerns and the government acted decisively.
- He criticized the Workers’ Party for abstaining on key votes and for not having concrete plans for constituencies they do not hold.

**6. Dangers of Race, Religion, and Foreign Interference in Politics**
- Lee spoke strongly against mixing race and religion with politics, referencing recent incidents and foreign attempts to influence Singapore’s elections.
- He called on Singaporeans to reject divisive politics and to uphold multi-racialism, meritocracy, and national unity.
- He warned that if Singapore’s politics becomes race- or religion-based, it would be disastrous, as seen in other countries.

**7. The PAP’s Track Record and Appeal for Support**
- Lee reminded the audience that the PAP has led Singapore for over 60 years, delivering stability, prosperity, and unity.
- He noted that even opposition parties now campaign on the assurance that the PAP will remain in government, which shows the trust Singaporeans have in the PAP.
- He urged voters not to “play games” with their votes or try to tactically balance opposition and government, but to vote for the party they truly believe will serve Singapore best.

**8. Closing Rallying Call**
- Lee ended by calling for unity and support for the PAP, emphasizing the need for honest, capable, and trustworthy leaders to guide Singapore through uncertain times.
- He led the crowd in cheers of “Majulah Singapura” and “Majulah PAP,” reinforcing the message of national progress and party unity.

**In summary:**  
Lee Hsien Loong’s speech was a call for Singaporeans to recognize the serious challenges ahead, trust in the PAP’s proven leadership and track record, reject divisive and identity-based politics, and vote decisively for a strong, united government to secure Singapore’s future.
