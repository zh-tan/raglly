## Summary of Gan Kim Yong’s Speech at Punggol GRC Rally (PAP, GE2025)

**Gan Kim Yong** began by expressing his honor at joining the Punggol team and his appreciation for the vibrant community and excellent amenities in Punggol. He acknowledged the hard work over the past 15-20 years by the PAP government and previous leaders to develop Punggol into one of Singapore’s most sought-after residential areas.

### Key Points from Gan Kim Yong’s Speech:

#### 1. **Commitment to Punggol Residents**
- Gan Kim Yong emphasized that, despite his national responsibilities as Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Trade and Industry, his first priority is always his residents. He stressed that being an MP comes before being a minister, and he is committed to serving Punggol residents with dedication.

#### 2. **Track Record and Ongoing Improvements**
- He highlighted the many achievements in Punggol, including:
  - The creation of the Punggol Digital District, which has generated over 28,000 jobs.
  - Expansion of childcare and infant care places (over 3,000 new places since 2020, with more to come).
  - The development of green spaces, parks, and recreational amenities, such as Punggol Waterway Park and the largest public library in Singapore.
  - The upcoming new sports stadium, which will include a 5,000-seat stadium, swimming pools, badminton courts, and more, scheduled to open in early 2026.

#### 3. **Future Plans for Punggol**
- Gan Kim Yong outlined concrete plans for further improvements, including:
  - Enhancing public transport: New Punggol Coast MRT station, new bus interchange, and two-car LRT trains to reduce congestion.
  - Maintaining and upgrading both the old and new bus interchanges, with the old interchange to be converted into a permanent interchange connecting two MRT lines.
  - Improving road networks, including a new bridge to Seletar Link to reduce traffic congestion.
  - Building more covered linkways for better connectivity, especially for students and during bad weather.
  - Addressing estate management and cleanliness, especially in newer estates like Northshore.

#### 4. **Support for Families, Seniors, and the Vulnerable**
- Gan Kim Yong recognized the challenges faced by young families, such as balancing work and caregiving, and the need for more support for caregivers and seniors.
- He promised to continue expanding active aging centers and senior citizen corners to reduce social isolation and support multigenerational families.
- He mentioned ongoing and future initiatives to support lower-income families, such as meal vouchers, CDC vouchers, and exploring affordable meal vending machines.

#### 5. **Jobs and Economic Resilience**
- As Minister for Trade and Industry, Gan Kim Yong spoke about national efforts to grow the economy, help businesses, and provide better jobs for Singaporeans.
- He referenced the Singapore Economic Resilience Task Force, which helps businesses and workers adapt to global challenges.
- He pledged to double down on reskilling and upskilling initiatives to prepare residents for the jobs of the future.

#### 6. **Personal Commitment and Reassurance**
- Gan Kim Yong addressed concerns about his age (66) and multiple responsibilities, assuring residents that he has the energy and commitment to serve Punggol fully.
- He stated, “Yes, I’m task force man. I have many tasks, but none more important than the task of looking after you.”
- He asked residents to give him and his team the opportunity to serve for another five years, promising to work as a team to care for every resident.

#### 7. **Call to Action**
- He concluded by urging residents to vote for the PAP team, emphasizing that the stakes are high for their welfare, jobs, families, and future.
- He promised to continue building on the good work of previous leaders and to serve Punggol with all his heart.

Gan Kim Yong’s speech focused on his commitment to Punggol residents, the PAP’s strong track record, concrete plans for further improvements in infrastructure, transport, family and senior support, and jobs.
