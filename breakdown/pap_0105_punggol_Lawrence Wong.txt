## Summary of Lawrence Wong’s Speech at PAP Punggol GRC Rally (GE2025)

**Opening & Acknowledgement:**
- Lawrence Wong expressed his happiness at being in Punggol for the final PAP rally of the campaign.
- He praised the PAP Punggol team (Gan Kim Yong, Janil Puthucheary, Sun Xueling, Yeo Wan Ling) as experienced, steady, and committed, highlighting their track record and deep ties to the community.

**Punggol’s Progress:**
- He noted Punggol’s transformation into a sought-after residential area with excellent amenities, green spaces, and the Punggol Digital District (PDD), which has created over 28,000 jobs.
- He attributed these achievements to 15–20 years of hard work and vision by successive PAP governments and teams.

**Listening to Residents & Local Initiatives:**
- Wong shared that he and the team have been actively engaging residents, listening to their concerns about estate management, cleanliness, transport connectivity, and amenities, especially in Northshore.
- He promised that, if elected, the team would prioritize resolving these issues, including improving covered walkways, public transport, and food options (e.g., new hawker centre opening soon).
- He highlighted ongoing and upcoming community support initiatives, such as meal vouchers, CDC and SG60 vouchers, and exploring affordable meal vending machines.

**Social Support & Jobs:**
- Emphasized the importance of supporting vulnerable groups, including the elderly and lower-income families.
- Stressed that the best way to help is to secure good jobs for residents, and highlighted national efforts to grow the economy, support businesses, and prepare workers for the future.
- Promised to further explore ways to support caregivers and seniors in Punggol.

**Addressing Concerns About Leadership:**
- Responded to concerns about Gan Kim Yong’s ability to serve Punggol given his national responsibilities, affirming that being an MP and serving residents is always the first priority for PAP ministers.

**The Stakes of GE2025:**
- Wong emphasized that this election is about residents’ welfare, jobs, families, and the future of Singapore.
- He urged voters to consider carefully who they trust to represent them and to lead Singapore through uncertain times.
- He acknowledged that some Singaporeans want more alternative voices in Parliament, but assured that opposition presence is already guaranteed and likely to grow.
- He warned that losing experienced ministers would weaken the government’s ability to serve and lead, drawing an analogy to losing key players in a football team.

**Why PAP:**
- Stressed that the PAP offers not just national programs but also innovative, customized solutions at the local level.
- Highlighted the importance of character in leaders—honesty, integrity, and putting Singapore first.
- Stated that the PAP is not perfect but is always upfront, learns from mistakes, and puts Singaporeans at the center of its work.
- Contrasted PAP’s approach of unity, action, and building up Singapore with the opposition’s focus on division and posturing.

**Vision for Singapore:**
- Reiterated his vision for a more caring, fair, and just Singapore, with support for every child, worker, family, and senior.
- Cited concrete policy changes already made in cost of living, education, housing, and healthcare.
- Called for unity and collective effort to weather global challenges and build a brighter future.

**Closing Appeal:**
- Urged residents to vote for the PAP for a brighter future for themselves, their families, and Singapore.
- Reminded everyone that every vote matters and that the outcome will decide the country’s future.
- Ended with a call for unity, integrity, and action—“Vote for the PAP. Vote for a brighter future. Majulah PAP, Majulah Singapura.”

**In summary:**  
Lawrence Wong’s speech was a rallying call for trust in the PAP’s proven leadership, local and national track record, and commitment to unity and progress. He urged voters to consider the stakes, the character of candidates, and the need for experienced leadership to guide Singapore through challenging times, promising that the PAP will always put Singaporeans first and work to build a better future together.
