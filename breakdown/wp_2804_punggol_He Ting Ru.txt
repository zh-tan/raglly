**Summary of He Ting Ru’s Speech at the Workers’ Party Rally, Punggol GRC, GE2025**

He Ting Ru, leader of the Sengkang GRC team and Workers’ Party MP, delivered the closing speech at the rally. Her key points and vision are as follows:

**1. Vision for a Healthier Singapore**
- She called for a “healthier Singapore,” not just in physical health, but in society, politics, and community.
- A healthier Singapore is one where people flourish, with clean air, healthy food, green spaces, fair wages, and strong communities.
- She emphasized that Singapore needs a healthier political system, where Parliament is strong, not just the government, and where the well-being of Singaporeans is at the heart of policies.

**2. Town Council Management as Proof of Capability**
- He Ting Ru highlighted the Workers’ Party’s successful management of Sengkang Town Council after GE2020, despite challenges such as merging two town councils and managing during COVID-19.
- She noted that Sengkang achieved clean audits, green bandings for cleanliness and governance, and improved performance, showing that opposition-run town councils can succeed.

**3. The Importance of a Strong Opposition**
- She argued that a strong Parliament with more opposition MPs means more time and space to scrutinize policies and push for those that benefit Singaporeans.
- She gave examples where WP’s presence and pressure led to government U-turns or improvements, such as the blocking of the NTUC Income sale, CPF housing rules, and cost-of-living relief.
- She stressed that the opposition is not there to oppose for the sake of it, but to keep the government accountable and policies balanced.

**4. The Dangers of Weak Opposition**
- He Ting Ru warned that taking opposition for granted is risky. She recalled that in 2015, WP nearly lost all elected opposition seats, which would have meant little to no check on the government.
- She questioned whether nominated MPs (NMPs) can truly hold the government accountable, especially when some NMPs later join the PAP.

**5. Call to Action**
- She urged voters not to risk losing elected opposition MPs again, but to vote for the Workers’ Party to build a healthier, more balanced Singapore.
- She called for support not just in Sengkang, but in Aljunied, Punggol, Jalan Kayu, East Coast, and other constituencies.
- She closed by emphasizing the need to work together for a stronger, healthier Singapore, able to face future challenges.

**In summary:**  
He Ting Ru’s speech focused on the need for a healthier Singapore, both in governance and society. She showcased the Workers’ Party’s track record in town council management, argued for the necessity of a strong opposition to keep the government accountable, and warned against complacency that could lead to a lack of checks and balances. Her rallying call was for voters to support the Workers’ Party to ensure Singapore remains fair, resilient, and ready for the future.
