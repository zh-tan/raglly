**Summary of Alfred Tan’s Speech at the SDP Sembawang GRC Rally (Singapore Election 2025):**

Alfred Tan, a candidate for Sembawang GRC under the Singapore Democratic Party (SDP), delivered a passionate speech focusing on the struggles faced by ordinary Singaporeans and small businesses due to the current government’s policies. Here are the key points he made:

1. **Rising Cost of Living and GST Increases:**
   - Alfred highlighted that Singaporeans are "bleeding" not because they are lazy or lack ideas, but because the government has stopped listening to them.
   - He criticized the PAP government for raising the GST right after the COVID-19 pandemic, during a period of inflation, when people were just starting to recover.
   - He questioned whether PAP MPs, including those from Sembawang, stood up for residents against these increases, concluding that they did not and simply followed party instructions.

2. **Need for Real Leadership:**
   - He argued that true leadership means listening to residents and standing up against harmful policies.
   - He promised that SDP MPs would speak up for residents, push for caring and humane policies, and not just act as "rubber stamps" for the government.

3. **Support for Small Businesses (SMEs):**
   - Alfred emphasized the importance of small businesses as the backbone of the local economy, contrasting them with multinational corporations (MNCs) that have little loyalty to Singapore.
   - He blamed government policies for high costs faced by SMEs, including GST, high rents, and expensive Certificates of Entitlement (COEs) for vehicles.
   - He called for government intervention to control land and rental costs, especially for government-owned properties, to help SMEs and keep food prices affordable.

4. **Housing Policy and HDB Prices:**
   - Alfred criticized the government for allowing resale HDB flat prices to soar, which in turn drives up the prices of new flats.
   - He explained SDP’s proposal for "non-open market" flats, which would be sold without including land costs and must be sold back to HDB, making them more affordable for Singaporeans.
   - He pointed out that this idea was proposed by SDP long before the Progress Singapore Party (PSP) existed, refuting PAP accusations that SDP copied PSP’s ideas.

5. **Town Management and Local Issues:**
   - Alfred promised better town management, including cleaner estates, improved flood management, quicker responses to estate issues, and safety patrols.
   - He criticized the current PAP MPs for being too busy or unresponsive to residents’ concerns.

6. **Civil Servants and Voting:**
   - He reassured civil servants that their vote is secret and that voting for the opposition is not a betrayal but an act of patriotism.
   - He emphasized that a good civil service should work with any elected government, not just the ruling party.

7. **Call to Action:**
   - Alfred urged voters to choose politicians who care, listen, and dare to speak up.
   - He encouraged residents to vote for SDP if they want a well-maintained town, real help for businesses, and MPs who will challenge unfair policies.
   - He closed by calling on voters to "vote with your mind, vote with your heart, vote for hope, vote for life, vote for change, vote SDP."

**In summary:**  
Alfred Tan’s speech was a rallying call for change, focusing on the rising cost of living, the struggles of small businesses, unaffordable housing, and the need for responsive, caring leadership. He positioned the SDP as the party that listens, stands up for ordinary people, and offers real solutions, urging Sembawang residents to vote for SDP for a better future.
