**Summary of Dr. Chee Soon Juan’s Speech at the SDP Bukit Panjang/Sembawang West Rally (Singapore Election 2025):**

Dr. Chee Soon Juan’s speech was a heartfelt and passionate address to the residents of Sembawang West, focusing on his journey, the challenges faced by opposition politicians, and his vision for the constituency and Singapore.

**Key Points:**

1. **Personal Journey and Commitment:**
   - Dr. Chee recounted his nine years of dedicated work in Bukit Batok SMC, highlighting his deep connection with the community and the pain of seeing the constituency dissolved and merged into a GRC.
   - Despite this setback, he emphasized that his commitment to serving residents remains unwavering, and he has now focused his efforts on Sembawang West.

2. **Ground Engagement and Dedication:**
   - He detailed the extensive outreach efforts by his team, including distributing flyers to every household and visiting all 108 blocks in Sembawang West within three weeks.
   - Dr. Chee praised his volunteers for their passion and hard work, noting that their motivation comes from a genuine desire to serve, not material rewards.

3. **Critique of PAP’s Campaign Tactics:**
   - Dr. Chee addressed personal attacks from PAP candidates, particularly accusations that he had “abandoned” Bukit Batok.
   - He called for a higher standard of political discourse, urging all candidates to focus on policies and issues rather than personal attacks, as young Singaporeans are watching and learning from their leaders’ conduct.

4. **Vision for Sembawang West:**
   - Dr. Chee promised to be a full-time MP, personally managing the town council rather than outsourcing it to profit-driven companies, ensuring cost savings and better care for residents.
   - He pledged to hold regular Meet-the-People sessions and town hall meetings to directly engage with residents and address their concerns.

5. **Community and Mental Health:**
   - He highlighted the lack of open green spaces in Sembawang West, connecting it to the broader mental health crisis in Singapore, especially among youth.
   - Dr. Chee advocated for more community spaces and green areas to improve residents’ well-being.

6. **Policy Focus:**
   - He reiterated the SDP’s commitment to tackling the high cost of living, job security, and healthcare affordability.
   - Dr. Chee assured residents that ongoing upgrading projects would continue smoothly under an SDP-managed town council.

7. **Call for Political Decorum:**
   - He urged PAP candidates to stop personal attacks and instead engage in meaningful debate on policies.
   - Dr. Chee invited his opponents to have a civil discussion over coffee about how to improve residents’ lives.

8. **Closing Appeal:**
   - Dr. Chee asked residents to vote confidently for him and the SDP, promising to make them proud and to transform Sembawang West into a caring, vibrant community.
   - He emphasized that voting is secret and encouraged voters not to fear supporting the opposition.

**Overall Message:**
Dr. Chee’s speech was a blend of personal reflection, policy proposals, and a strong call for ethical, people-centered politics. He positioned himself as a dedicated, full-time MP who will prioritize residents’ needs, foster community spirit, and bring compassion and accountability back to Singaporean politics. He urged voters to choose hope, courage, and real change with the SDP.
