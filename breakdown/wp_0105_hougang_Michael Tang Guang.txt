**Summary of Michael Tang Guang’s Speech at the Workers’ Party GE2025 Hougang Rally**

Michael Tang Guang, a Workers’ Party (WP) candidate, delivered a speech aimed especially at undecided voters and those considering voting for the ruling People’s Action Party (PAP). Here are the key points he made:

**1. Speaking to the Undecided**
- Michael acknowledged the loyal WP supporters but focused his speech on those who are still unsure or leaning towards the PAP, asking for patience from the crowd as he addressed their concerns.

**2. Challenging the Status Quo**
- He noted that many Singaporeans say they are “satisfied” with the way things are, but he urged them to ask if they can imagine more and better for Singapore.
- Drawing from his experience working with large, dominant companies, he observed that such organizations often become complacent, lose their innovative edge, and protect the status quo—paralleling this with the PAP’s current approach.

**3. Critique of PAP’s Performance**
- Michael cited examples of government inefficiency and lack of imagination, such as spending $400,000 to rename Marina Bay (and keeping the same name), spending $40 million to fix the SimplyGo system, and relying on vouchers as the main response to the cost-of-living crisis.
- He argued that the PAP is no longer the party of bold, forward-looking ideas and needs “hammering away from the outside” to keep them on their toes.

**4. The Value of Competition and Alternative Voices**
- He emphasized that even brilliant individuals cannot do their best work if they are boxed in, and that competition and new ideas are necessary for progress.
- Voting for the WP, he argued, is a way to introduce competition and ensure the government works harder for the people.

**5. Addressing Fears of Change**
- Michael reassured voters that electing WP MPs would not mean losing current amenities or plans in their constituencies. Instead, residents would benefit from both WP MPs and PAP grassroots advisers, leading to “double” the events and services—a “buy one, get one free” scenario.
- He cited Aljunied as an example where this has worked well.

**6. The Impact of an Opposition Vote**
- He asserted that every vote for the WP ensures the PAP works harder for all Singaporeans, regardless of the election outcome.

**7. Confidence in the WP Team**
- Michael highlighted the strengths of the WP team in Tampines (his constituency), including professionals with deep roots in the community and relevant experience, and expressed confidence that they would serve residents well.

**8. Call to Action**
- He ended by rallying the crowd to vote for the Workers’ Party and to encourage friends and family to do the same, emphasizing that change can only happen if everyone works together.

**In summary:**  
Michael Tang Guang’s speech was a direct appeal to undecided and cautious voters, encouraging them to imagine a better Singapore, to see the value of competition and alternative voices in Parliament, and to dispel fears about losing out if they vote for the opposition. He positioned the Workers’ Party as a force for positive change, accountability, and innovation, and called on Singaporeans to be courageous and vote for hope, not just for the status quo.
